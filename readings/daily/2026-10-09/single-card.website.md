*friendship, celebration, community, joy, support*

okay, so the three of cups is here to remind you about the good stuff. the kind of good stuff that doesn't happen alone — it's the shared laugh, the clinking glasses, the spontaneous toast to something perfectly ordinary but suddenly significant. you've been working, planning, perhaps even worrying, and this card says: pause. look around. who's with you?

this isn't just about a party, though a party would certainly fit. it's about the deep nourishment you get from your people, the ones who get it, who show up, who make the small moments feel big and the big moments feel possible. it’s about creative collaboration, too — the sparks that fly when ideas bounce between kindred spirits. it’s the joy of being truly seen, truly supported. a simple pleasure, really. and you deserve it.

## the sky

**Moon in Virgo** (5th house) — the moon in virgo wants things accounted for
**Venus in Libra** (7th house) — venus in libra wants everything to be beautiful and fair
**Sun in Libra** (7th house) — the sun in libra highlights your desire for partnership and balance

## what it means together

this three of cups isn't just a suggestion to have a good time — it's a very clear directive to notice the *structure* of your good times, and the people who make them. with the moon in virgo in your fifth house, your emotional nature is currently looking for the details, the logistics of joy. you might find yourself quietly observing the dynamics of your friendships, appreciating the small, thoughtful gestures that truly hold a group together. it’s about making sure your celebrations aren't just spontaneous, but also *well-supported*.

and with both the sun and venus in libra, sitting right there in your seventh house of partnerships — well, that's a big, bright spotlight on how you relate to others, how you seek harmony, and how you define fairness within your most important connections. the three of cups is asking you to lean into that libra energy: to actively cultivate balance in your friendships, to ensure everyone feels seen and valued, not just by you, but within the larger community you share. it's about making space for everyone's cup to be full, ensuring the joy is truly collective.

so yes, celebrate. gather your people. but do it with a conscious awareness of the give-and-take, the subtle ways you sustain each other. your heart wants things to be accounted for, yes, but also beautiful and fair. this moment is about recognizing those who truly lift you up, and perhaps, making a deliberate effort to lift them back. it feels good, doesn't it? that kind of reciprocal joy.
