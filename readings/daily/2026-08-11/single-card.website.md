*rebellion, dogma, restrictive beliefs, empty ritual, nonconformity*

you're at a crossroads, where the usual ways of doing things — the institutions, the traditions, the dogma — are being called into question. the hierophant, reversed, suggests that the system you've been following, or the beliefs you've been holding onto, may not be serving you like they used to. it's possible that you've been going through the motions, performing empty rituals, or adhering to restrictive beliefs that are stifling your growth. this card can indicate a need to break free from conformity, to rebel against the status quo, and find a more authentic, living relationship to your beliefs.

the hierophant, in its upright position, is all about tradition, conformity, and spiritual guidance — but when it appears in reverse, it's like the mask is being ripped off, revealing the underlying emptiness or hypocrisy. you may be feeling a sense of disillusionment, or a growing awareness that the things you've been taught, or the ways you've been conditioned, are not entirely true. this can be a difficult, but ultimately liberating, experience.

it's a time to question everything, to challenge your assumptions, and to seek out new, more meaningful ways of connecting with the world, and with yourself. the hierophant, reversed, is not about destroying the old, but about creating a new, more authentic path — one that's based on your own inner wisdom, rather than external authority.

## the sky
**the moon in aquarius** — the moon wants humanity, and a sense of community, to guide your emotional decisions. 
**venus in leo** — venus wants your heart to be filled with creativity, and generous self-expression. 
**mars in scorpio** — mars wants you to dive deep, and penetrate to the heart of any matter that's been troubling you.

## what it means together
when you combine the energy of the reversed hierophant with the transits in the sky, you get a sense of revolution, of turning things upside down, and creating a new order. the moon in aquarius is all about humanitarian pursuits, and creating a sense of community — but the hierophant, reversed, suggests that this community should be based on shared values, rather than rigid dogma. venus in leo adds a sense of creativity, and generosity, to your relationships — but the hierophant, reversed, reminds you that true creativity, and generosity, can only come from a place of authenticity, and nonconformity. mars in scorpio, meanwhile, is all about penetrating to the heart of the matter — and the hierophant, reversed, suggests that this penetration should be directed inward, toward your own beliefs, and assumptions.
