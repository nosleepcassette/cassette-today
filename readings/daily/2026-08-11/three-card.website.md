*scattered will, aggression, lack of control, delay, tradition, teaching, guidance, ritual, intuition, mystery, inner knowing, stillness*

You're coming from a place of disarray — The Chariot in reverse shows that your usual drive and direction have been disrupted, and you may have been acting on impulse rather than intention. This can be a frustrating and disorienting experience, especially if you're someone who values control and progress. The good news is that this chaos is not a permanent state, and you're now in a position to reassess and regroup. The Hierophant in the present position suggests that you're being called to connect with a source of wisdom, guidance, or tradition that can help you find your footing again. This could be a person, a practice, or an institution that can offer you a sense of stability and direction. The key is to be open to receiving guidance and to be willing to listen and learn. 
You need a pause. 
The High Priestess in the future position indicates that the next step is not about taking action, but about tuning in to your inner wisdom and trusting your intuition. This is not a time for outer exploration, but for inner listening and stillness.

## the sky
**the moon in virgo** — the moon wants things accounted for, and is scrutinizing every detail of your emotional landscape. 
**venus in leo** — venus is shining bright, and your creative spark is ignited, but also potentially overwhelming. 
**mars in gemini** — mars is restless, and your energy is scattered, easily distracted, and prone to mental chatter.

## the thread
The combination of these cards and transits suggests that you're in a period of transition and reorientation. The chaos of the past is giving way to a desire for guidance and direction, and you're being called to connect with a source of wisdom that can help you navigate this uncertain time. However, the moon in virgo is also cautioning you to be careful and discerning in your pursuit of guidance, and to make sure that you're not just following blindly. The High Priestess is urging you to trust your intuition and listen to your inner voice, rather than getting caught up in outer noise and distractions. 
You're looking for a way out of the noise. 
The key is to find a balance between seeking guidance and trusting your own inner wisdom. This is not a time for dogmatic thinking or rigid adherence to tradition, but for open-minded exploration and a willingness to listen and learn. The venus in leo is adding a creative spark to the mix, and suggesting that you have the power to ignite your own inner light and find your own way, rather than relying solely on external sources of guidance. 
It's a complex dance, but one that you're capable of navigating.
