*bondage, attachment, materialism, temptation, shadow*

The Devil is where desire stops being information and starts running the household. You know the pattern is costing you, but some part of you keeps calling the cost necessary — necessary for love, safety, money, attention, control, or relief. The chain may be real, but the story you tell about why you cannot remove it is where the lock lives.

This card does not arrive to shame you for having appetites. Wanting things is not the sin here. The problem is handing your agency over to the wanting, then pretending the arrangement was unavoidable. You may be caught in a relationship, habit, spending pattern, fantasy, or power dynamic that feeds you just enough to keep you from leaving hungry.

Look at what you keep defending. Look at what you call complicated because the simpler truth would require a choice. The Devil is blunt, and frankly, a little rude about it. Freedom begins when you stop negotiating with the thing that benefits from your confusion.

## the sky

**Sun in Sagittarius** (2nd house) — your sense of worth wants more room, more truth, and less of its identity tied to what you own, earn, or can prove.

**Moon in Gemini** (9th house) — your feelings need movement and new information, but curiosity can become another way to keep circling the answer.

**Mercury in Scorpio** (2nd house) — your mind is digging beneath the story around money, self-worth, and security to find what you have been refusing to name.

## what it means together

The Devil meets a sky focused on value, security, and the stories that hold them in place. Sagittarius wants freedom, but freedom is not the same as running toward the next shiny exit. Gemini wants options, but more options will not save you if every road leads back to the same compulsion. Mercury in Scorpio is the one willing to look under the floorboards.

Ask what you are actually afraid would happen if you stopped feeding this. Not the polished answer. The ugly one. Would you feel unwanted? Poor? Ordinary? Unprotected? Would you have to admit that the thing you keep chasing cannot give you what you want?

Name the exchange clearly. What does this attachment give you, and what does it take? Then remove one point of access today — one payment, one message, one excuse, one ritual that keeps the chain polished and intact.

The door is not locked in the way you think it is. It is just heavy, and you have been using both hands to hold it shut.
