## hexagram 17: Following

```text
17: Following
---- ----
---------
---------
---- ----
---- ----
---------
```

*lake over thunder, willing alignment, discernment, recuperation*

choose what gets to set your pace. **Following, hexagram 17**, isn't an instruction to let the loudest person decide. it asks what you are already following: a trustworthy principle, the rhythm of your body, the desire to belong, or an argument that keeps giving you another notification. something is organizing your attention. you have a say in what that something is.

the judgment reads, “FOLLOWING has supreme success. / Perseverance furthers. No blame.” following becomes useful when it stays attached to what is right, not merely what is popular or exciting. adapting to another person can be generous. surrendering your judgment to keep their approval is a different arrangement. the favorable language doesn't remove the need to tell those apart.

the image is thunder in the middle of the lake. the instruction is surprisingly undramatic: at nightfall, go indoors for rest and recuperation. following includes following the time. there is a point at which another hour of argument, work, or explanation is no longer courage. it's refusing to recognize that the day's available capacity has ended.

that matters beside today's friction. you don't need to extract a final resolution from every conversation before allowing yourself to stop. you can say when you will return to an issue, or decide it doesn't need more of your attention. rest doesn't invalidate conviction. sometimes it is the practical condition that lets conviction remain something other than irritability with a manifesto attached.

### changing line 3: choosing one attachment changes another

the third line changes: “If one clings to the strong man, / One loses the little boy.” the saved continuation explains that through following one finds what one seeks, and perseverance is favorable. the old gendered figures point toward a choice of attachments, not a rule about whose age or gender makes them worthy of your trust.

in daily terms, choosing a steadier commitment may mean giving up a more immediate reward. you can't always preserve approval from every direction while acting on what you know matters. you might lose the pleasure of the last word when you choose a useful conversation. you might lose the comfort of a familiar role when you choose a boundary. loss by itself isn't evidence that you chose badly.

be careful not to turn this into a fantasy of superior people and disposable people. the line is more helpful when you examine the pattern you're feeding. what strengthens your ability to live honestly? what keeps you occupied but smaller? attach yourself to the practice, agreement, or relationship that can withstand a truthful answer. let a tempting distraction lose a little of its claim on your day.

### changing line 5: sincere in the good

“Sincere in the good. Good fortune.” the fifth changing line gives the choice a center. not winning, not appearing above the conflict, not convincing everyone that you're kind. sincerity asks whether your action stays connected to what you actually value when the room becomes uncomfortable.

choose a value you can make concrete. fairness might mean letting someone finish and also asking them to hear you. care might mean declining a commitment you would otherwise fulfill resentfully. honesty might mean naming a limit without inventing an excuse. a value that only exists as a flattering description of yourself doesn't have much to steer with. give it an action.

read the two changing lines together: choose an attachment that supports your better judgment, and keep faith with the good it is meant to serve. this isn't rigid loyalty to a person or institution. if the thing you're following asks you to betray the principle that made it worth following, you are allowed to notice the mismatch.

lines three and five changing relate to **Abundance, hexagram 55**. this is a possible development, not a forecast of money, popularity, or a dramatic reward for setting a boundary. its image joins thunder and lightning: movement with clarity. the judgment's instruction to be like the sun at midday concerns being present to fullness rather than spending the whole bright hour mourning its eventual end.

for today, abundance can be read as the capacity to use what becomes available when your attention is better aligned. a free evening. a clear agreement. enough mental space to enjoy something rather than monitor a conflict. none is guaranteed. the relating hexagram asks how you would meet that opening if it came, instead of immediately filling it with another version of the same struggle.

## the synthesis

name what you want to follow, then give it a limit and a practice. if it's fairness, choose the next fair action. if it's care, include your capacity in the calculation. if it's truth, stop adding explanations after you've made the point clear. tonight, follow the image literally enough to end something on time. the argument can stop being the metronome for your whole day.
