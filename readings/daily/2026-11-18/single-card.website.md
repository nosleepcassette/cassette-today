*recovery, release, reconciliation, healing, lingering grief*

Something in you is beginning to unclench. The Three of Swords reversed does not pretend the wound never happened — it shows the part where the pain starts moving again, instead of sitting in the same place and demanding a daily tribute.

You may be ready to forgive, but forgiveness does not require pretending the harm was acceptable. It can mean releasing your grip on the version of the story that keeps you bleeding. It can mean admitting that you still miss someone, still feel angry, or still wish things had gone differently, without turning those feelings into instructions.

This is healing in the unglamorous middle. You are not magically over it. You are processing it honestly enough that it no longer has to control every decision.

## the sky

**Sun in Scorpio (2nd house)** — your sense of self and security is going underground to find what is real, rather than what looks convincing from the outside.

**Moon in Pisces (6th house)** — your feelings are porous and easily stirred, so ordinary work, care, and usefulness may carry more emotional weight than usual.

**Mercury in Scorpio (2nd house)** — your mind wants the truth beneath the explanation, especially around your worth, your resources, and what you will no longer bargain away.

## what it means together

The reversed Three of Swords meets a Scorpio sky with one demand: stop decorating the wound and look at what it changed. The Sun and Mercury are turning attention toward self-worth and material safety, which means this grief is not only about who hurt you or what ended. It is also about the parts of yourself you abandoned while trying to be chosen, understood, or kept.

The Pisces Moon makes the day softer, but not necessarily easier. You may feel someone else’s absence in small tasks, familiar routines, or the quiet after a message goes unanswered. Let the feeling exist without building a shrine to it. Eat something. Answer the practical email. Put the money where you can see it. Healing is allowed to have admin.

There is room for reconciliation, but begin with yourself. Let the old version of the bargain expire. You do not have to keep proving that you were hurt in order for the hurt to count.
