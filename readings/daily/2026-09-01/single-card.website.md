*cycles, fate, turning point, change, opportunity*

here it is — the wheel turns, whether you push it or not. you're entering a cycle where things are shifting, often outside your immediate command. it's not personal, it's just how the cosmic gears grind, how the seasons change, how the tides come in and go out. you can try to hold it still, brace against the current, but that's like telling the ocean to stay put. it won’t listen.

this spin brings with it a specific kind of energy, a timely opening or a sudden reversal. sometimes it’s a stroke of luck, a door you didn’t know was there swinging wide open. other times, it’s the universe reminding you that what goes up must eventually, gracefully or otherwise, come down. the point isn't to control the wheel itself — that's a fool's errand — but to understand its motion, to know when to ride the wave and when to brace for impact. life changes. always.

## the sky

**Mercury in Libra** (1st house) — your thoughts are currently weighing options, seeking fairness and equilibrium.
**Moon in Virgo** (10th house) — your emotional compass wants things accounted for, finding comfort in practical adjustments.

## what it means together

so, the wheel is spinning, and you're caught in its current, but your mind wants to find the perfect balance. mercury in libra is all about weighing consequences, trying to find the most equitable path forward, even when the path itself feels like it's being paved by forces beyond your control. you're looking for the sweet spot, the fair deal, the way to make sense of the inevitable shift. it’s a good instinct, really — to bring some deliberation to the chaos.

meanwhile, your moon in virgo is quietly, diligently, trying to sort out the emotional fallout, to make practical adjustments to whatever this turning point brings. you want to understand the mechanics, the details, the how-to of adapting. this isn't about grand philosophical acceptance yet; it's about the tangible steps, the small shifts you can make to feel grounded as the world tilts. the wheel of fortune says things are moving, and your sky says, "okay, but how do we organize this new reality?" it’s about finding your footing, even if the ground beneath you isn't quite stable.
