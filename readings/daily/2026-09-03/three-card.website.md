## the thread

this entire journey — from the blinding clarity of the sun, through the quiet, necessary work of the five of cups reversed, to the potential sharpness of the queen of swords reversed — is deeply informed by that tower energy that kicked things off. something broke open. something was revealed. and while the sun brought light to the rubble, and the five of cups reversed helps you gather what’s salvageable, the queen of swords reversed asks you to be incredibly mindful of the shards.

your past success and insight were real, but they came at a cost, or perhaps they were the *result* of a necessary dismantling. you’re moving through the grief of what had to fall away, learning to accept the new landscape. the danger isn't in seeing things clearly; it's in letting the pain of what you now understand fester into a bitter, isolating shield. don't let your wisdom become a wall. your discernment is a gift, but it needs to be tempered with compassion, starting with yourself. the task is to integrate the truth without letting it harden your heart.
