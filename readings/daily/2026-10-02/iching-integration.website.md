**Adorning (#22)**
*Adorning, MOUNTAIN, FIRE, KEEPING STILL*

fire at the foot of the mountain: that’s the image of hexagram 22, adorning. you see the flames licking up, illuminating the base of the rock, not burning through it or transforming it, but highlighting its contours. it's about the beauty of the surface — how things present themselves, how they're arranged, the grace notes that make an impression. this isn't about deep structural change, or the core truth of the mountain, but about how it appears, how it's perceived. it's the art of presentation, the power of form.

the judgment for hexagram 22 is direct: *grace has success. in small matters it is favorable to undertake something.* this isn't a call to superficiality, not quite. it’s an acknowledgment that sometimes, the way something looks, the way it’s framed, is exactly what’s needed to move forward. it’s about the polish, the packaging, the careful arrangement that allows something to be seen in its best light. but notice the caveat — "in small matters." this grace, this adorning, isn’t for everything. it has its place, its specific utility.

this hexagram names a time for refinement, for attention to detail in presentation. it asks you to consider how you’re showing up, how your work is perceived, or how a situation is being framed. it’s about the aesthetics of your endeavors — the visual appeal, the clarity of communication, the elegance of your approach. think of it as tidying up, putting things in order, making sure the surface reflects care and intention. it’s not about grand gestures or radical overhauls, but about the nuanced art of making things *look* right, *feel* right, at least on the outside. it suggests a posture of careful arrangement, a focus on the external presentation.

the iching for this reading presents hexagram 22 in its static form, without any changing lines. this means the energy of adorning is the primary, unchanging message for your day. there isn't a specific path of transformation or a destination hexagram to consider; the lesson lies squarely in the present moment, within the confines of grace and presentation itself. you're meant to sit with this energy, to understand its full implications without the added layer of movement or evolution.

## the sky

the sky today is quiet. there are no dominant planetary transits making big demands or pushing specific agendas. this means the internal energies—the dynamics of your tarot cards and the grounded wisdom of the iching—are the primary drivers of your experience. it's a day less about external astrological pressure and more about what's unfolding within your personal landscape, how you're choosing to engage with the themes presenting themselves from your own inner world.

## synthesis

you start the morning with the three of pentacles reversed, which immediately brings up questions of recognition and collaboration. maybe you’re feeling unseen in your efforts, or like your contributions aren’t quite landing. the skills are there, the work is being done, but the external validation—the "adorning" of your efforts with praise or proper compensation—feels off. it’s a quiet frustration, a sense that the structure isn't quite supporting the talent within it. you know what you’re capable of, but the world isn’t reflecting that back to you in the way you need.

this is where hexagram 22, adorning, becomes your guide. the three of pentacles reversed tells you the external recognition isn't there. the knight of wands reversed shows you why: your energy, while potent, isn't being presented or channeled effectively. it's not about lacking the skill or the drive, but about how that skill and drive are *perceived*. the iching asks you to focus on the grace notes, the presentation, the clarity of your message. it’s not about changing who you are, but about how you frame it.

you're being asked to step back from the raw, unrefined energy of the reversed knight and consider the form. how can you make your efforts more appealing, more understandable, more *gracious*? the eight of wands pushed you into action; now, the three of pentacles reversed and the knight reversed are asking you to pause and refine your approach. this isn't about being fake; it’s about strategic presentation. you have the talent, you have the drive. the future king of wands is waiting for you to harness that power with intention and a touch of elegance.

this means looking at the "small matters" where grace has success. it’s about clearing up current affairs—the image of the hexagram—making sure your professional dealings, your communications, your personal presentation are all in order. you don't need to reinvent the wheel, but you need to polish it. make sure your work is visible, that its value is clear, that your efforts are communicated with a certain finesse. the king of wands doesn't just stomp into a room; he enters with purpose, with a presence that commands respect. you can cultivate that by first paying attention to the details, the adorning touches that elevate your substance. it’s about making sure your flame illuminates the mountain, rather than just flickering wildly.
