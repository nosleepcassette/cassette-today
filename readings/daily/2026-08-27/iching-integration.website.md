**Long Lasting (#32)**
THUNDER over WIND, the AROUSING
*Long Lasting, Duration, Perseverance*

thunder and wind: the image of DURATION. thus the superior man stands firm and does not change his direction.
This image describes the deep, unchanging rhythms of nature — the persistent, arousing force of thunder, combined with the penetrating, steady influence of wind. It’s not about a sudden, violent storm, but the constant, unwavering presence of these forces. They endure. They reshape the landscape not by a single blow, but by continuous, patient effort. This hexagram asks you to embody that same unwavering presence, to stand firm, to commit to a direction, and to not be swayed by every passing gust. It’s about building something that lasts, and becoming someone who lasts.

*DURATION. Success. No blame. Perseverance furthers. It furthers one to have somewhere to go.*
The Judgment speaks directly to the power of consistency. This isn't about merely *surviving* a situation; it’s about actively *thriving* through sustained, well-directed effort. Success here isn’t a quick win, but the cumulative result of showing up, day in and day out, for what truly matters. There’s no fault in sticking with what works, in refusing to chase fleeting trends, or in maintaining a commitment that’s proving its worth. But the key, the I Ching reminds you, is having a clear destination. Perseverance without a purpose is just running in place. With a clear "somewhere to go," your staying power becomes truly transformative.

Long Lasting names a time when the core of something—a relationship, a project, a personal ideal—needs to be maintained, nurtured, and held firm. It’s about the kind of commitment that builds foundations, the kind that creates a legacy, not just a momentary splash. What in your life is currently asking for this steady, unwavering attention? What structures are you laying down now that need to be truly durable, capable of withstanding the inevitable shifts and challenges? This hexagram is a call to align your energy with a deep, enduring rhythm, to cultivate a dynamic persistence that sees you through to the finish line, and beyond.

The fourth line of the hexagram moves:
**Line 4: No game in the field.**
This line delivers a sharp, practical warning. You are out there, perhaps, fully equipped and ready to hunt, to achieve, to make things happen—but there’s simply nothing to catch. The field is empty. Your energy, your skill, your powerful intent are all primed, yet the target isn't present, or the conditions are wrong for a successful pursuit. The message is clear: don't waste your shot. Don't expend your valuable resources, your focus, your time, on a pursuit that won’t yield lasting results right now. Sometimes, the most lasting thing you can do is to observe, to wait, to intelligently redirect your formidable energy towards a more promising field. It's not about giving up; it’s about intelligent conservation and strategic patience.

**Relating Hexagram 46: Growing Upward (升)**
When Hexagram 32 shifts, when you heed the warning of Line 4 and choose your field wisely, you move into Growing Upward. This is where the patient, consistent effort of DURATION finds its profound reward. It’s a journey of steady, deliberate ascent. *PUSHING UPWARD has supreme success. One must see the great man. Fear not. Departure toward the south Brings good fortune.* This isn't about sudden, dramatic leaps, but about consistent, incremental progress. The "great man" here isn't necessarily another person; it's the ideal, the higher self, the guiding principle you're aspiring to embody. Don't be afraid to rise, to grow, to commit to the forward movement that brings you into the light. This hexagram is your destination — a place of sustained, successful growth, built on the foundations of your enduring commitment.

Maybe you’re misdirecting it, or perhaps you're being a little too clever, a little too focused on the show rather than the substance. There’s incredible potential here, but it’s currently out of alignment, or you’re trying to conjure something that isn't really there. You have all the tools, but what are you building? And for whom?
