the high priestess steps in right here, offering a vital counter-balance to that overwhelm. she isn’t asking you to fight harder or to give up entirely, but to *pause* — to choose stillness over striving. she’s telling you that the answers aren't out there in the fray, but deep inside you, waiting to be accessed through quiet reflection. this isn't inaction born of fatigue; it’s about *intentional* inaction, about gathering your internal resources, about listening to the subtle currents of your own wisdom.
