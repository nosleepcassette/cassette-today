*delays, low energy, false start, hesitation, creative block*

the ace of wands reversed is the match that won't strike. the spark is there — you can feel it, the potential hasn't disappeared — but something's smothering it before it catches. maybe it's fear, maybe it's exhaustion, maybe it's the voice that says "not yet" every time you reach for the thing you actually want to make.

reversed aces are always about delay, but not the kind imposed from outside. this is the internal no. the creative impulse meeting the wall of your own hesitation. you know what you want to start, what you want to reach for, but the energy won't gather. it scatters. it fizzles.

the reversal doesn't mean the fire is gone. it means you've lost contact with it. buried it under practicality, second-guessing, the weight of everything else. the work here is reconnection — not forcing the spark, but clearing the space for it to land.

## the sky

**Sun in Sagittarius** (2nd house) — the sun wants freedom and meaning, but it's currently occupied with questions of worth and security.

**Moon in Cancer** (10th house) — the moon is protective, emotional, pulling inward even while sitting in the most public part of the chart. security needs are loud right now.

**Mercury in Scorpio** (2nd house) — your thoughts are sharp and suspicious, circling around what you're worth and what you own, stripping away pretense to get to what's real.

## what it means together

the reversed wand is suffocating under the weight of the second house. two planets there — sun and mercury — both grinding on self-worth, material security, what you have versus what you need. the sun in sagittarius wants to expand, to leap, to find meaning in the big gesture, but it's stuck calculating whether you can afford the risk. mercury in scorpio is doing the same math, only darker — penetrating, suspicious, asking if the spark is even real or just another false start you can't afford.

meanwhile the moon in cancer is in your tenth house, the most visible part of your chart, and it wants to retreat. protective, emotional, security-focused. that's the smothering energy — the instinct to pull back, to stay safe, to not expose yourself before you're sure.

the ace reversed isn't a no. it's a "not while you're white-knuckling the safety rail." the fire's there, but you're holding your breath. let the worth questions settle for a minute. the spark doesn't need permission from your bank account or your anxious moon. it just needs you to stop checking whether it's allowed to exist.
