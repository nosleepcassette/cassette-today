you're standing your ground — the Seven of Wands is a card of conviction, persistence, and refusing to surrender a hard-won advantage. it's about holding your position when pressure rises and opposition tests your resolve. you've worked hard to get where you are, and you're not about to let someone or something take that away from you. 
it's a delicate balance — between being open to feedback and being stubborn. 
the key is to know when to dig in and when to yield.

the Seven of Wands is not about being aggressive or confrontational — it's about being clear and firm in your intentions. you're not looking for a fight, but you're not backing down either. 
you're willing to take a stand, even if it means standing alone. 
it's a brave and courageous thing to do.

## the sky
**the moon in virgo** — the moon in virgo wants things accounted for, and is paying close attention to the smallest details. 
**mars in libra** — mars in libra is trying to balance competing desires and needs, and is seeking harmony and equilibrium in all relationships. 
**venus in leo** — venus in leo is shining bright, and is craving attention, admiration, and adoration.

## what it means together
the Seven of Wands, with the moon in virgo, mars in libra, and venus in leo, suggests that you're being called to stand up for what you believe in, even if it means going against the crowd. 
it's not about being reckless or impulsive — it's about being thoughtful and intentional in your actions. 
you're being asked to take a close look at your motivations, and to make sure that you're acting from a place of integrity and authenticity. 
it's a time for courage and conviction, and for trusting your inner voice. 
you've got this.
