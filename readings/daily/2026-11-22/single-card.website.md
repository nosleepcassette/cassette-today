*bitterness, coldness, cynicism, cutting words, isolation*

You already know what's happening. You've become very good at seeing through people — their motives, their performance, the small lies they tell themselves about their own value. That's real skill. It's also become a weapon, and lately you've been holding it with both hands.

The reversed Queen of Swords isn't losing her mind. She's using her clarity to hurt, or to keep people at a distance where she can call it honesty. Pain is doing the talking. The tell is that after you say the sharp thing, you feel lighter, and the other person feels smaller. That's not the Queen at her best. That's armor with a sharp edge.

So here's the work: you get to keep the insight and lose the bitterness. Say the true thing without the knife in it. Notice the difference between a boundary and a wall. If you've been withholding warmth as a strategy, that's the card asking you to stop. You don't have to go soft. You just have to stop being cold on purpose.

## the sky

**Sun in Sagittarius** (2nd house) — your sense of self and self-worth runs on open air and new horizons, and you want your security to feel like freedom, not a locked drawer.

**Moon in Taurus** (7th house) — you want your closest relationships calm, steady, and uncomplicated, and you show that side of yourself more freely than you'd think.

**Mercury in Scorpio** (2nd house) — your thinking is digging under the surface of money, worth, and identity, looking for what's being withheld, and it has no interest in surfaces.

## what it means together

Here's the shape of it: two of these three transits sit in your second house, so the Queen reversed is pointed straight at your own sense of value. The Sun wants to feel expansive and worth something. Mercury in Scorpio wants to strip that self-image down to the studs and see what's actually holding it up. Meanwhile your Moon in Taurus wants peace in partnership, and the reversed Queen keeps making it easy to say something cold and call it precision.

The card hinges on one thing: whether your sharp mind serves your warmth or your resentment. Watch which one is running today. When Mercury digs and finds something uncomfortable about money, worth, or what you honestly think you're worth, the temptation is to go cold and get it over with. Try the other move. Let the Sagittarius Sun have some air. Let the Taurus Moon have its quiet evening. Say the accurate thing without making anyone pay for it.

Keep the clarity. Leave the blade in the drawer.
