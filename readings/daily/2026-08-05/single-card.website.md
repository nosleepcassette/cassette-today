*jealousy, volatility, insecurity, selfishness, domination*

you know that feeling when someone's confidence doesn't sit right — not because it's loud, but because it's brittle. the queen of wands reversed is the version of yourself you don't want to admit exists: the one who needs the room to look, the one whose warmth curdles into control when she doesn't get it. she's still magnetic, still capable of lighting up a space — but the fire's gone from a hearth to a hair-trigger.

this card shows up when your charisma has started doing double duty. you're not just showing up, you're defending. every interaction becomes a stage, and you're checking the exits while you perform. the queen reversed doesn't mean you've lost your power — it means you're using it to keep people at the exact distance where they can admire you but never actually touch you. the jealousy isn't just about others. it's about the version of you that doesn't need to try so hard.

## the sky

**Sun in Leo** (5th house) — the sun's at home in leo, amplifying your need to be seen and celebrated, but the 5th house can turn that into a performance you can't stop.  
**Mercury in Virgo** (6th house) — your mind's running diagnostics on everything, including yourself, and it's finding flaws you didn't know you were looking for.

## what it means together

here's the thing about the queen of wands reversed under a sun-in-leo transit: you're getting exactly the attention you think you want, and it's making you feel worse. the sun's in its own sign, handing out visibility like candy, but the queen's reversed fire turns that into a hunger that can't be satisfied. every compliment lands like a reminder that you might not deserve it. every moment of being unseen feels like a verdict.

mercury in virgo isn't helping. it's running a constant commentary on every interaction — *did you sound desperate? was that too much? why did you say it like that?* — and the queen reversed hears all of it as evidence. the volatility isn't coming from outside. it's the gap between the performance you're maintaining and the exhaustion of maintaining it.

what would it look like to let someone see you without the staging? to let the fire just be warm instead of impressive? the queen of wands upright trusts her heat. reversed, she's afraid the flame won't be enough unless she makes it a spectacle. the sky's saying you don't need the spectacle. you need the rest that comes after you stop proving you're worth watching.
