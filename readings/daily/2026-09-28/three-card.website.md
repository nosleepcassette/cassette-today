## the sky

**Mercury Retrograde in Leo** (5th house) — mercury retrograding in leo has you rethinking how you express yourself, especially in creative or playful ways.

## the thread

the high priestess started this with a quiet nudge: *pay attention to what’s unsaid.* because what’s happening is, you fought so hard for what you have (seven of wands), and now you’re clutching it (four of pentacles). it’s a natural, almost instinctual response to past struggles. you secured your spot, and now you’re determined to keep it. the moon in virgo in your career house just reinforces this — you want every detail accounted for, every asset cataloged, every boundary clearly marked. it feels responsible, prudent.

but saturn in pisces is quietly, subtly, eroding the very definition of those boundaries around your resources. it’s asking you to reconsider what you *truly* value, and how you earn it, how you sustain it. the ground you’re standing on, the one you fought so hard to defend, might be shifting beneath your feet in ways you can’t quite see yet. this isn't a threat, it’s a re-evaluation. and mercury retrograding in leo is making you rethink how you express your value, how you put yourself out there. are you holding onto an old image of yourself, or of your resources, that no longer serves the deeper current?

the ace of pentacles reversed in your future suggests that the new opportunity, the fresh start, is there. it is. but it’s struggling. it can't quite take hold because the soil isn't ready, or the conditions aren't right. this isn’t about a lack of potential, it’s about a lack of reception. if you’re clenching so tightly to what you have, if you’re so focused on defending past gains, how can you open your hand to receive something new? the high priestess, whispering from the deep, knows that sometimes the greatest security isn’t in holding tight, but in trusting the flow. in allowing for a little mess, a little uncertainty, for something radically new to grow. it’s time to decide if the security you’ve built is a fortress, or a cage.
