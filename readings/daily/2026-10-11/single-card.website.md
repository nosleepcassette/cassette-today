*transition, passage, healing, moving on, support, distance*

the six of swords is the crossing. you're out of the worst of it, but not on the other shore yet, and the water between here and there still has some chop in it. the swords come with you. that part matters. this card doesn't promise you leave your thoughts, your history, or the sharp stuff behind. you carry it, and you move anyway.

it's a quiet card. no fanfare, no clean break, no slammed door. just steady movement away from something that wasn't working toward conditions you can actually breathe in. if today feels bittersweet, that tracks. leaving a hard place still means leaving a familiar one.

pay attention to the boat, too. in most versions of this card, somebody else is rowing. that's the *support* in the keywords, and it's easy to skip past. you don't have to steer every inch of this transition yourself. if there's a person, a system, or a routine that can carry some of the weight, today is a good day to let it.

sixes are about regaining flow after strain. in swords, that strain lives in the mind: the looping, the rehearsing, the arguments you keep having with people who aren't in the room. the six says the loop can loosen now. not because you solved it, but because you put some *distance* between yourself and it.

## the sky

no significant transits came through on today's chart. the sky is quiet: no hard aspects to push against, no planet demanding center stage.

the card carries its own astrological signature, which is worth working with on a day like this. **Mercury in Aquarius** is the traditional correspondence for the Six of Swords: thinking that's cool, detached, and future-facing. it's a mind that can step back far enough to see the whole map instead of staring at the one square it's stuck on.

## what it means together

a quiet sky and a card about leaving quietly. there's no outside current dragging you today, and nothing in the chart to blame or credit. if you're moving, it's because you decided to.

so use the stillness. take the Mercury-in-Aquarius move and look at your situation from a little higher up. what are you still circling that you could just stop circling? what are you trying to fully process before you'll let yourself move on? the six doesn't require you to resolve everything first. it asks you to get in the boat, sit down, and accept help with the rowing.

the reading's bottom line: clarity comes from cutting through, not circling. you don't need a grand gesture. you need to keep moving toward calmer water and let the distance do some of the healing.
