## the thread

This morning's Six of Swords set the undertone. Transition, passage, a boat moving away from rough water toward somewhere calmer. You were already mid-crossing before the spread was laid down. That matters, because the three cards read like a warning about what can pull you off course halfway across.

The Three of Pentacles is the earth you pushed off from, practical and collaborative and real. The Seven of Cups is the reflection on the water. It's beautiful and it moves, and it's very easy to stop rowing and stare at it. The Lovers reversed is what happens when you steer toward the reflection instead of the shore: you end up aligned with something that looked right and never was. The card correspondences say the same thing. The Three carries Mars in Capricorn, disciplined effort. The Seven carries Venus in Scorpio, desire with an undertow. The Lovers carries Gemini, two minds and a split.

The hinge is the present card. The Seven of Cups doesn't have to go anywhere bad. It's asking for discernment, and you already have the tools for that; the Three of Pentacles gave them to you. Go back to how you built the last thing well. You had a plan, you had people you trusted to check your work, and you cared about quality over shine. Use the same criteria on the cups. Which of these would survive contact with the actual work? Which one would the people who know your craft look at and say, yes, that's you?

Pick that one. Let the rest stay pretty on the shelf. The Lovers reversed is a forecast, not a sentence, and the way you change the forecast is by making a choice your values actually agree with.
