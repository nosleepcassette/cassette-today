**Eliminating (#43)**
*LAKE, HEAVEN, THE JOYOUS*

the lake has risen up to heaven — that’s the image for hexagram 43, Eliminating. imagine the water, usually contained, now reaching for the sky. it’s not a gentle mist, it’s a force. a breakthrough, yes, but also a boundary broken. it means what’s been held back, what’s been submerged, is now making itself known, demanding to be seen, to be dealt with. it’s a moment of clarity, a moment where things can no longer stay hidden or unaddressed.

the judgment puts it plainly: *BREAK-THROUGH. One must resolutely make the matter known At the court of the king. It must be announced truthfully. Danger. It is necessary to notify one's own city. It does not further to resort to arms. It furthers one to undertake something.* this isn’t about whispering your truth or hinting at a problem. it’s about a decisive, public declaration. but you can’t just blurt it out. you have to be resolute, yes, but also strategic. there’s danger, so you alert your allies, your “own city.” and crucially, no arms. no aggression. this isn’t a fight, it’s an unveiling. it’s about making a cut, severing ties, or eliminating what’s no longer viable, so something new can begin.

this hexagram names a time for decisive action, for separating yourself from what has run its course, or for excising a problem that’s become too large to ignore. it demands a posture of clear-eyed assessment, of courage to speak what needs to be said, to do what needs to be done. it’s about drawing a line in the sand, but doing so from a place of integrity, not hostility. you’re not looking for a fight; you’re looking for resolution. you’re creating space.

the second line shifts the focus a bit, giving you a warning wrapped in reassurance. *A cry of alarm. Arms at evening and at night. Fear nothing.* you’ll likely feel that initial jolt of fear, the alarm bells ringing when you contemplate this breakthrough, this necessary elimination. you might feel vulnerable, exposed, as if you need to guard yourself constantly. the line acknowledges this natural reaction, but then tells you directly: don’t give in to it. fear is present, yes, but it doesn’t have to dictate your actions. be prepared, sure, but don’t let terror paralyze you. the alarm is a signal, not a stop sign.

the sixth line, the very top of the hexagram, offers a stark contrast, a final, urgent caution. *No cry. In the end misfortune comes.* this is the consequence of failing to heed the call of Eliminating. if you suppress that alarm, if you refuse to make the matter known, if you choose silence when a declaration is needed—that’s where the real trouble lies. it’s a warning against inaction, against letting things slide. the misfortune doesn't arrive as a sudden blow, but as the slow, inevitable decay of what should have been cut away. it's the cost of avoiding the necessary breakthrough, of not speaking your truth when it’s demanded. you can’t simply wish a problem away. you must act.

## relating hexagram: 13 — Seeking Harmony (同人)

when the changes in hexagram 43 complete their movement, when the breakthrough is made and the elimination is complete, you arrive at hexagram 13, Seeking Harmony. this is where you’re headed, the destination after the difficult but necessary work of clearing. the judgment for this one is clear: *FELLOWSHIP WITH MEN in the open. Success. It furthers one to cross the great water. The perseverance of the superior man furthers.* this isn’t just a footnote; it’s the payoff. the hard lines drawn, the truths spoken, the things eliminated — they weren't for destruction's sake. they were to create the conditions for genuine connection, for open and honest fellowship. after you’ve made the cut, after you’ve cleared the air, you can truly connect. it’s about moving from a place of internal struggle and necessary severance to one of outward, authentic collaboration. crossing the great water, undertaking something significant, becomes possible, becomes *furthering*, once the path is clear. this is where your perseverance lands you: in a space of shared purpose, free from the entanglements you just eliminated.
