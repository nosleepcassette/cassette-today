## the thread

the spread is saying: you put down the burden. that was the right move. but now you're in danger of turning the relief into a new problem -- not by doing too much, but by thinking too much about whether you're doing it right.

this morning's card was Three of Pentacles: craftsmanship, teamwork, skill. that's the instruction for how to handle the page of pentacles without sliding into the nine of swords. treat the learning as craft, not as a test. work with others instead of trying to figure it all out alone in your head. the burden you carried in the past wasn't sustainable. the future doesn't have to be anxiety -- but it will be if you let the page's curiosity turn into obsessive second-guessing.

so here's the move: treat today's work as practice. one task, done with care, in coordination with someone else if possible. the nine of swords shows up when you isolate with your worry. the three of pentacles is the antidote -- collaboration, planning, skill applied to something concrete. do that today, and the future stays open instead of collapsing into fear.
