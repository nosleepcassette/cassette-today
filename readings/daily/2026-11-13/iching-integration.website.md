**Initiating (#1)**
*HEAVEN, THE CREATIVE*

the first hexagram is made up of six unbroken lines. these unbroken lines stand for the primal power, which is light-giving, active, strong, and of the spirit. the hexagram is consistently strong in character, and since it is without weakness, its essence is power or energy. the judgment says: "THE CREATIVE works sublime success, Furthering through perseverance." when an individual draws this oracle, it means that success will come to him from the primal depths of the universe and that everything depends upon his seeking his happiness and that of others in one way only, that is, by perseverance in what is right.

the image: "The movement of heaven is full of power. Thus the superior man makes himself strong and untiring." since there is only one heaven, the doubling of the trigram Heaven indicates the movement of heaven. one complete revolution of heaven makes a day, and the repetition of the trigram means that each day is followed by another. this creates the idea of time and duration.

both trigrams are Heaven (the Creative -- pure yang, strength, sky, the father principle). the doubling intensifies this quality -- it fills the entire structure of the hexagram. two lines are changing at positions 3 and 6.

line 3: "All day long the superior man is creatively active. At nightfall his mind is still beset with cares. Danger. No blame." a sphere of influence opens up for the great man. his fame begins to spread. the masses flock to him. his inner power is adequate to the increased outer activity. there are all sorts of things to be done, and when others are at rest in the evening, plans and anxieties press in upon him. but danger lurks here at the place of transition from lowliness to the heights. many a great man has been ruined because the masses flocked to him and swept him into their course. ambition has destroyed his integrity. however, true greatness is not impaired by temptations.

line 6: "Arrogant dragon will have cause to repent." when a man seeks to climb so high that he loses touch with the rest of mankind, he becomes isolated, and this necessarily leads to failure. this line warns against titanic aspirations that exceed one's power. a precipitous fall would follow.

this movement tends toward Hexagram 58, Joyful (兌): "THE JOYOUS. Success. Perseverance is favorable." the joyous mood is infectious and therefore brings success. but joy must be based on steadfastness if it is not to degenerate into uncontrolled mirth. truth and strength must dwell in the heart, while gentleness reveals itself in social intercourse.

## the tarot surface

this morning's card was **Four of Pentacles**: security, control, conservation, stability. it gives the day its first visible instruction: check whether the protection has become the problem, whether you're holding tight because it's valuable or because letting go would mean admitting the situation has changed.

the three-card spread: **The High Priestess** in the past -- intuition, mystery, inner knowing. a period of receptivity, waiting, sensing, knowing something but not yet able to act on it.

**Knight of Wands** in the present. action, adventure, boldness, passion. fire of fire -- pure drive, pure momentum. the action breaking through -- finally, the ability to charge toward what you want.

**Four of Wands (Reversed)** in the future. tension at home, instability, incomplete milestone. the celebration interrupted by instability. the foundation isn't ready. the charge is real, but the ground underneath needs work before it can hold the weight of arrival.

---

## the synthesis

the cards show receptivity in the past, bold action in the present, instability ahead if the foundation isn't strengthened. the hexagram shows the primal pattern: Initiating, the Creative, pure yang, six unbroken lines. this is power itself, the movement of heaven, the force that never stops. together they say: yes, charge forward -- but know the difference between power and arrogance.

line 3 describes where you are now: a sphere of influence opens up. his fame begins to spread. the masses flock to him. his inner power is adequate to the increased outer activity. that's the knight of wands in full motion -- creatively active all day long, still beset with plans and anxieties at nightfall. there's danger here, at the place of transition from lowliness to the heights. ambition can destroy integrity. the masses can sweep you into their course. true greatness is not impaired by temptations -- but only if you recognize them as temptations.

line 6 warns where this goes if unchecked: the arrogant dragon will have cause to repent. when a man seeks to climb so high that he loses touch with the rest of mankind, he becomes isolated, and this necessarily leads to failure. titanic aspirations that exceed one's power lead to a precipitous fall. that's the four of wands reversed -- the foundation can't hold the weight, the celebration turns into instability, the charge meets a reality that isn't ready.

the relating hexagram, Joyful, is what happens if you get this right: success, the joyous mood that brings people together. but it's conditioned: joy must be based on steadfastness, not uncontrolled mirth. truth and strength must dwell in the heart, while gentleness reveals itself in social intercourse. in other words, the Creative power is real, but it has to be paired with the receptivity of the high priestess. pure yang without yin turns into arrogance. the knight without the foundation turns into the reversed four of wands.

so here's the practical move: you're right to charge forward. the initiating hexagram says the power is there, the moment is real. but line 3 says danger lurks at the transition from lowliness to the heights, and line 6 says arrogance leads to repentance. the four of pentacles this morning was about control -- not letting go of what you have. the hexagram is saying: you can let go of the tight grip, but you can't let go of discernment. charge forward, yes -- but stay in touch with the ground, stay in touch with the people, stay in touch with the reality that the foundation needs to be built as you go. creative power all day long, but at nightfall, let your mind be beset with cares. that's not weakness -- that's what keeps the dragon from becoming arrogant.
