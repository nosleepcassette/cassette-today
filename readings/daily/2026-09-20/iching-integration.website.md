**Beginning (#3)**
WATER, THUNDER
*Beginning, WATER, THUNDER, THE ABYSMAL*

you’re standing at the threshold of something immense, something just forming, and the image of it—clouds and thunder—is exactly what it feels like: a storm brewing, potent and wild, before the rain even touches the earth. it’s the sound and fury of creation itself, the raw energy that hasn’t found its shape yet. this isn't a gentle dawn, it’s the tumultuous birth of a world. the superior man, the hexagram tells you, brings order out of this kind of confusion. you are that superior man, or woman, or person. your role here isn't to be swept away, but to conduct the chaos, to give it form.

the judgment arrives like a pronouncement, clear and unyielding: *difficulty at the beginning works supreme success, furthering through perseverance. nothing should be undertaken. it furthers one to appoint helpers.* this isn't a suggestion, it's a map. it tells you the struggle you’re feeling right now isn’t a sign you’re doing it wrong, but a necessary part of the process. the "supreme success" is baked into this difficult start, a promise waiting on the other side. but the path there isn't action. "nothing should be undertaken" is a hard stop for anyone eager to push forward. this moment isn't for forcing, it’s for gathering, for careful observation. and you don’t have to do it alone. the universe is literally telling you to call in your people, to find your guides, your support, your counsel.

this hexagram names the very first breath, the moment before differentiation, when everything is potential and nothing is solid. it’s the raw clay before the potter’s hands, the seed just pushing through the soil. it demands a posture of patient awareness, of deep listening, not forceful intervention. the situation isn't a problem to be solved by brute force, but a complex, living system that needs to be understood before it can be guided. it sees the world you’re navigating as pregnant with possibility, yes, but also utterly unformed. there’s a deep, primal power here, but without direction, it could just as easily dissipate or overwhelm. your job is to be the gentle, firm hand that helps it find its way. this isn’t about building, not yet—it’s about laying the foundation for what *will* be built, acknowledging the inherent struggle in that first, vital push.

## the sky

**Moon in Libra** (1st house) — the moon in libra wants things to feel balanced, to find a sense of fairness in every interaction.
**Sun in Virgo** (12th house) — the sun in virgo is meticulous, wanting to analyze every last detail, even the hidden ones.
**Mercury in Libra** (1st house) — mercury in libra encourages you to think about diplomacy, to weigh your words carefully, to seek harmony in your communications.

## synthesis
