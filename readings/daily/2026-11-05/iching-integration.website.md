## hexagram 22: Adorning

**Adorning (#22)** — 賁 (bì)  
*Mountain above Fire; form, grace, the limits of appearances*

```text
6  ───────
5  ─── ───
4  ─── ───
3  ───────
2  ─── ───
1  ───────
```

Fire at the foot of the mountain makes its contours visible. The mountain does not become a different mountain because it is illuminated; the light lets you see its shape. Adorning is concerned with this relationship between the thing itself and the form through which it is perceived. Beauty, order, and care can reveal what matters. They can also be used to obscure it.

The judgment, translated here as Grace, favors modest undertakings. The image recommends attending to current affairs but warns against deciding serious controversies by appearance alone. A clear message, a comfortable room, or an orderly plan can make a difficult matter easier to approach. None of those things settles the underlying question by itself. A polished explanation is not evidence that an arrangement is fair.

This distinction is especially useful when you are trying to make an unstable situation look manageable. Presentation can help you find the next step. It can also become a place to hide from the numbers, the disagreement, or the feeling that needs attention. The reading asks you to use form in service of substance, rather than asking form to do a job it cannot do.

### the stable cast

There are no changing lines in this cast and no relating hexagram. The answer stays with Adorning itself. That means the six lines in the diagram are not separate changing-line instructions, and there is no second figure here promising a particular outcome. Spend time with the judgment and image instead of treating the absence of movement as a problem to solve.

A stable cast does not mean you are trapped, or that nothing in your life can change. It means this reading's emphasis is the present condition: how you arrange, name, and illuminate what is already in front of you. The smallness in the judgment is useful. You can improve a surface without confusing that improvement for the whole repair.

Consider a message you have been putting off. It might need a subject line, a concise request, and the relevant facts. Those are forms of care; they make the message easier to receive. If the question is whether an agreement is safe or affordable, however, the quality of the prose cannot decide it. Read the terms. Ask the hard question. Let presentation support the truth rather than replace it.

## the tarot

**Ace of Cups (reversed) — focus** describes an emotional current that feels blocked, guarded, or difficult to receive. There may be care available and still be a reason you cannot simply relax into it. Adorning does not ask you to decorate over that difficulty with a cheerful expression. It suggests that a gentle container can help: a familiar place to sit, a manageable conversation, or a request that doesn't require you to explain every feeling at once.

**Three of Wands (upright) — past** recalls a wider horizon and effort already moving outward. You had reason to imagine growth, exploration, or a return on what you set in motion. A good plan may have helped you make that first move. Now the question is whether the plan still describes the conditions you are meeting, or whether its attractive outline needs to be revised.

**King of Swords (upright) — present** brings principled judgment. He asks for facts, ethical clarity, and language that makes the decision understandable. In conversation with Adorning, his gift is separating a persuasive presentation from a sound argument. He can use an orderly document without mistaking its orderliness for proof. He can also hear a feeling without requiring it to arrive in perfect sentences.

**Five of Pentacles (upright) — future** raises the possibility of material hardship or exclusion. It is a prompt to notice vulnerability and seek support, not a guarantee that deprivation is coming. If something essential is missing, a tidier room or more reassuring wording can help you cope, but the missing thing still needs to be named. Care has a practical side.

## what form can and cannot do

There is dignity in arranging the immediate environment when you are overwhelmed. Clearing a chair, writing down the amount due, or putting a supportive contact where you can find it can make the next action possible. This is Adorning at a human scale: a small form that helps you meet what is real. It is not trivial just because it does not solve everything.

There is also a point where making things look fine becomes another demand on a depleted person. You do not have to produce a beautiful account of your struggle before someone is allowed to help. The reversed Ace may find a simple request more possible than a full emotional disclosure. The King can keep that request specific: “Can you help me check this bill?” or “Can you sit with me while I make the call?”

The Three of Wands and Five of Pentacles hold a tension between expansion and security. A plan may be exciting and still require a sober look at resources. If the numbers don't work, changing the plan is not a failure of imagination. It is how the imagined future remains accountable to the person who has to live in it.

## the synthesis

The tarot describes the movement from an outward-looking past through a present decision toward a possible test of support. Adorning adds a question about the surface of that movement: what helps the truth become visible, and what merely makes the situation look finished? The distinction matters when the guarded heart wants protection and the practical mind wants an immediate answer.

Choose one small improvement and one substantive action. Make the improvement something that genuinely eases the task: organize the papers, put on a light, or draft the opening sentence. Make the substantive action fit the actual issue: check the terms, ask for material help, or name a boundary. One gives the other a workable form.

A little beauty is allowed even when things are difficult. It does not need to justify itself as a cure. Let it be a way to stay present with the work, and let the work include whatever is still uncomfortable to look at.
