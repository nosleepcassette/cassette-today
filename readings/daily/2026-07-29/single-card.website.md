*curiosity, vigilance, ideas, communication, candor*

You're holding a question you haven't quite asked out loud. That's the Page of Swords — the part of you that's been circling something with your eyes narrowed, turning it over in your head at 2 a.m., testing how it sounds when you say it to yourself in the dark. This card is mentally quick, but not frantic. It's the sharp intake of breath before you speak. The moment you realize you have something to say — and you're not sure if it'll land, but you're going to say it anyway.

The Page is young energy, but not naive. It's the kind of youth that knows exactly how much it doesn't know, and finds that thrilling rather than terrifying. There's an appetite for truth here, and it's not polite. It wants to ask the question that's been dodged. It wants to read the fine print. It wants to test an idea in the open air — to see if it holds up.

## the sky

No major transits today — the sky is holding its breath, letting you have the floor. The planets aren't shouting over you. That's rare. Use it.

## what it means together

So the whole day is this: a clean, uncluttered moment to speak your piece. The Page of Swords doesn't need a perfect argument — it needs a start. A sentence. A text you've been drafting and deleting. A question you've been too polite to ask.

You don't have to be ready. You just have to be honest. That's the Page's whole deal — candor over polish, curiosity over certainty. The air is thin and clear today. Your thoughts can travel further than usual. Say the thing. See what happens. You can always refine it later — but you can't unsay what you never said.
