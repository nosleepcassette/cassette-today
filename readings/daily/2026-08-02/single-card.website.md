*avoided disaster, fear of change, lingering crisis, denial, internal upheaval*
you're likely feeling a sense of relief, like you've dodged a bullet — the tower, reversed, can indicate that a collapse or upheaval has been delayed, or maybe even partially avoided. but here's the thing: just because the disaster hasn't struck yet, doesn't mean it's not still looming. the truth is, the underlying issues are still there, simmering just below the surface. it's possible you've been able to temporarily avoid the consequences of your actions, or the universe has given you a bit of a reprieve — but don't think you're off the hook that easily. the tower, even in reverse, is still a call to reckoning. 
it's a card that says: don't ignore the warning signs. 
you can't just sweep things under the rug and expect them to stay there.

## the sky
**the moon in virgo** — the moon wants things accounted for, and is pushing you to get your ducks in a row. 
**venus in leo** — your sense of self-worth is tied up in your ability to create, and express yourself authentically. 
**mars in gemini** — your energy is scattered, and you're having trouble focusing on any one thing.

## what it means together
the tower, reversed, is like the universe giving you a timeout — a chance to get your act together before things really hit the fan. and with the moon in virgo, you're being prompted to take a hard look at your life, and get things in order. it's not about being perfect, it's about being honest with yourself. the venus in leo is a reminder that your self-worth isn't defined by external validation — you need to be creating, and expressing yourself in a way that feels true to you. and mars in gemini is just making everything feel a bit more frantic than it needs to — try to focus on one thing at a time, and don't get too caught up in the distractions. 
it all feels a bit much, maybe — but the tower, reversed, is a chance to take a breath, and get things sorted before the storm hits. 
you've got this.
