*transition, passage, healing, moving on*

you’ve been on a boat, haven’t you? a quiet, necessary departure from some rough waters. the six of swords in your past tells a story of moving on, of leaving behind what was no longer serving you — even if it felt a little bittersweet to go. it’s not always about grand, dramatic exits; sometimes it’s just the steady, supported glide away from the shore, toward calmer, if unfamiliar, seas. a quiet healing, an internal passage. you did that. but the six of cups reversed, setting today’s undertone, whispers that maybe not everything was truly left behind. a part of you might still be clinging to the old shore, to the familiar — even the familiar pain. a ghost on the deck.

## the thread

the thread pulling through these cards is all about movement — or the resistance to it. you started with a necessary departure, a six of swords pushing you toward peace, but the six of cups reversed suggests the emotional baggage didn’t all get left on the dock. you carried some of it with you, didn't you? that clinging to the past, that immaturity in fully letting go, is what strains the star's light in your present. it’s hard to see the future clearly, hard to feel truly hopeful, when one eye is still fixed on what’s behind you.

your present moment, represented by the star reversed, isn't about losing hope entirely, but about recognizing that your current approach to healing might be incomplete. it hinges on that final release. you can’t fully step into the new, calmer waters if you’re still trying to paddle back to the old ones. the energy you’re expending on that internal tug-of-war is draining the very faith you need to move forward. it’s a quiet sabotage.

and if that internal conflict isn't resolved, if that attachment isn't truly severed, the knight of wands reversed in your future suggests a kind of reactive impulsiveness. you might lash out, or charge ahead without a clear plan, mistaking frantic activity for actual progress. it’s a warning against burning bridges just to feel like you’re doing something, or letting frustration derail a journey that truly began with good intentions. the path to sustained progress, to that future peace, requires you to put down the baggage from the past, completely. you can't carry everything. decide what stays and what goes, for good.
