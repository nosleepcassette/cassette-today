*exposure, confession, self-deception, accountability, sloppy plans*

you've pulled the seven of swords, reversed — which, to be frank, feels less like a quiet, sneaky exit and more like a loud, clattering fall. the swords, once held close and out of sight, are now scattered. everyone sees them. your plans, or perhaps someone else's, were never going to stay hidden, were they? this isn't about getting away with anything anymore; it's about what happens when the light finally hits.

it's a moment of reckoning, plain and simple. the burden of keeping things under wraps, of carrying those secrets, has become too heavy. maybe you're the one confessing, laying out the truth you've been clutching. or maybe it's you realizing you've been living under a spell of your own making, deceiving yourself about the reality of a situation. either way, the jig is up. the energy here is less about regret and more about the sheer relief of dropping the weight. it's tiring, pretending.

you might be looking at a situation where someone else's sloppy plans are coming to light, and you're caught in the crossfire of their exposed machinations. or maybe those sloppy plans were yours, and now you have to face the music. the universe has a funny way of making sure all the pieces eventually show themselves. it insists on accountability.

## the sky

## what it means together

with the seven of swords reversed, the real question isn't *if* the truth will come out, but *how* you'll handle it when it does. or perhaps, how you'll handle the truth you're finally admitting to yourself. this card is a spotlight, and it's shining directly on the areas where honesty has been...flexible. you're being asked to step into that light, to own what's happening, even if it feels uncomfortable. it's the moment where all the little evasions, the half-truths, the things you whispered only to yourself, are laid bare.

this isn't necessarily a bad thing, you know. think of it as a necessary clearing. sometimes, the mess has to be fully visible before you can truly clean it up. the self-deception aspect is particularly potent here — it's easy to rationalize, to justify, to convince yourself that your intentions were pure, even when your actions were anything but. this card says: look again. look harder. what have you been telling yourself that isn't quite true? the universe is offering you a chance to reset, to build something real, but it starts with seeing things as they actually are, not as you wished them to be. it's a deep breath. then, a step forward.
