## the card

**The Sun (Reversed)**

*temporary cloud, low energy, delayed success, self-doubt, muted joy, burnout*

the sun reversed is showing up as your focus today, and it's not here to punish you — it's here to name something. that brightness, that vitality, that sense of being seen and certain and moving forward without hesitation? it's dimmed. not gone, just muted. you're running on a lower charge than usual, and the card is asking you to acknowledge that without spinning it into a catastrophe.

this isn't failure. it's fatigue. the core of what the sun promises — joy, clarity, life force — is still there, but right now it's harder to access. maybe you're second-guessing yourself in ways you don't usually, or the things that usually feel easy are taking more effort than they should. success isn't off the table; it's just delayed, or harder to feel confident about. the reversed sun often shows up when you're operating under a kind of low-grade doubt, the kind that makes everything feel a little heavier than it needs to be.

burnout is part of this picture. not the dramatic kind, necessarily, but the quieter version where you're still moving but nothing is lighting you up. you're doing the work, checking the boxes, but the warmth has drained out of it. that's not a character flaw. it's a signal that something needs rest, or adjustment, or honest acknowledgment. the sun at its best is about being fully alive and visible. reversed, it's asking you to look at what's blocking that — whether it's internal (how you're treating yourself) or external (conditions you're trying to push through that genuinely aren't working).

the good news is that this is temporary. the sun reversed doesn't suggest permanent darkness; it's a cloud passing over. but while it's here, it's worth asking what made the cloud gather in the first place. are you overextending? are you operating in a context that doesn't actually value what you bring? are you pushing yourself to perform brightness when what you need is permission to be dim for a minute?

the card isn't telling you to force optimism or fake energy. it's telling you to recognize where the vitality has gone, and to make space for it to return on its own terms. rest is not defeat. slowing down is not failure. sometimes the most honest thing you can do is admit that today, the light is low — and that's enough.
