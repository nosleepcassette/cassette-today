## the thread

that controlling, overbearing energy from your past — whether it was yours or someone else’s — has left its mark, hasn't it? it taught you what doesn't work, what happens when certainty trumps wisdom. the fool in your present moment is your brave, beautiful, perhaps slightly naive response to that. it's the decision to leap, to start fresh, to abandon the old, rigid structures for something entirely unscripted. and it’s a necessary break.

but that morning’s page of swords, reversed, it’s the background noise, isn’t it? the defensiveness, the whispers, the internal or external chatter trying to pull you back, to make you question this leap. it’s the lingering echo of past judgments, or the fear of new ones. it’s the part of you that wants to protect itself from another scorching, another overbearing force.

your future, however, is asking you to transcend that. strength isn't about defending yourself from the world; it’s about meeting it with an open heart and an iron will. it’s about finding that inner power that doesn’t need to prove anything, that doesn’t need to control, that doesn’t need to be right. it’s the integration of your wildness, your courage, and your compassion, allowing you to walk this new path with grace, not just grit. this isn't about taming the beast, you see. it's about befriending it.
