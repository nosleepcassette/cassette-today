## the thread

this spread tells a story of transformation, from the quiet release of a past burden to the sometimes-frustrating dance of the present, ultimately leading to a future of clear-sighted expansion. the undertone of the morning’s page of wands — that initial spark of exploration and enthusiasm — is the thread weaving through it all.

you began this journey with a curious spirit, a willingness to explore new territories, even if that exploration led you through difficult emotional landscapes. the reversed five of cups shows you emerging from that terrain, not unscathed, but certainly changed, having found a measure of peace. the grief doesn’t vanish, but its grip loosens, making room for new possibilities.

that initial, eager exploration (page of wands) set you on a path that led to both deep feeling and, currently, this sense of scattered energy. the chariot reversed isn't a condemnation, but a signal: your will needs re-alignment. it’s the friction point between letting go of the past and stepping into the future. you're holding onto the reins, but the horses are a bit unruly. this is a necessary stage, a moment to re-evaluate where you’re truly headed, and why.
