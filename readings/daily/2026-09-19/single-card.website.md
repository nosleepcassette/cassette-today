*setbacks, resistance, bad timing, repetition, lack of control*

when the wheel of fortune turns backwards, it doesn't mean the wheel has stopped — it just means the ride feels a little bumpy, maybe even like it's going in reverse. you’re probably sensing a distinct lack of forward momentum, or worse, a frustrating loop. this isn't a halt; it's a recalibration, or perhaps, a stubborn refusal to recalibrate. you might be experiencing what feels like persistent setbacks, those moments where you take two steps forward only to be yanked one or two steps back. it’s tiring, isn't it?

there’s a palpable sense of resistance here, either from external circumstances or, more subtly, from within yourself. things aren't flowing. the timing feels off, consistently so, as if the universe is deliberately misplacing your keys right when you need to leave. this card often points to repetitive lessons, situations that keep resurfacing until you've truly integrated the message. it's not trying to punish you — it's just really, really insistent.

you might feel a profound lack of control, which is the wheel's way of reminding you that some things are simply beyond your immediate command. but the real question isn't how to force the wheel to spin faster or in a different direction; it's how you can adjust your stance, your perspective, to move *with* the turning, even if it’s currently feeling like a grind.

## the sky

**Saturn Retrograde in Pisces** (6th house) — saturn retrograde in pisces in your sixth house means your daily grind feels like slogging through mud.
**Mercury in Scorpio** (12th house) — mercury in scorpio in your twelfth house keeps your deepest thoughts hidden, even from yourself.
**Mars Square Uranus** — mars squaring uranus brings sudden jolts and unexpected resistance when you try to move forward.

## what it means together

this feeling of the wheel turning against you — or just not turning *for* you — is amplified by the current sky. saturn retrograde in your sixth house is making every routine, every small task, feel like an uphill battle. no wonder you're sensing setbacks; your daily efforts are meeting an unexpected friction, a heavy, watery resistance that seems to slow everything down. it’s not that you're not trying; it’s that the ground beneath you feels less stable than usual.

and mercury hidden in scorpio in your twelfth house? that's where the internal resistance might be lurking. there are thoughts, perhaps fears or old patterns, deep in your subconscious that are subtly influencing your actions, or inaction. you’re wrestling with an inner current you can't quite name, and it’s contributing to the feeling of a lack of control, making the wheel’s backward spin feel even more disorienting. you're fighting shadows.

then mars squaring uranus throws in the wild card, the unexpected jolts that embody the "bad timing" this wheel reverse brings. just as you think you’ve found your footing, something unpredictable shifts beneath you. this isn’t about stopping the inevitable, but about finding your balance on a moving surface. acknowledge the resistance, look for the hidden currents, and instead of trying to force the wheel, practice moving with its current, even if it means a temporary detour. sometimes the most effective way to gain control is to release the illusion of it first.
