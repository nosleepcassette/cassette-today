*nostalgia, kindness, innocence, reunion, memories*

The Six of Cups brings a memory close enough to touch. It might be a person, a song, the shape of a kindness, or a version of yourself you haven't visited in a while. Nostalgia can be a source of warmth without being a reliable instruction to go backward.

Ask what the memory actually contains. Was it the person you miss, the freedom you felt near them, or a small ritual that made a difficult time bearable? The answer can help you carry something useful forward without rebuilding the whole situation. You might miss being playful without needing to return to the place where you learned to be careful.

This card also points to generosity on a human scale. A thoughtful note, an old recipe shared, a moment of undivided attention: none needs to become a grand reunion. If contact with someone from the past is complicated or unsafe, you can honor what was good without contacting them at all.

There is room here for the younger parts of you, but not an obligation to idealize childhood. Some memories are mixed; some are painful. Kindness might mean believing what you remember, allowing uncertainty, or offering yourself a comfort you didn't reliably receive then.

Choose one gentle thing worth keeping and give it a place in the present. Play the song, make the food, call a trusted friend, or simply remember without trying to resolve the whole story. The past can be a source of tenderness without becoming the address you have to live at.

If a memory brings sadness as well as warmth, you don't have to select the acceptable half. Missing a moment and knowing you cannot return to it can both be true. Give the contradiction a little room.
