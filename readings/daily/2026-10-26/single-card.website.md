*transition, passage, healing, moving on, support*

you’re in motion, even if it feels like a slow float — a quiet boat ride across water. the six of swords tells you the worst of the turbulence is behind you, or at least, you’ve picked up your things and you’re headed away from it. this isn’t a grand escape, not a sudden flight. it’s a deliberate, supported passage from one state to another. you might feel a pang of sadness for what you’re leaving, a bittersweet farewell to a situation that, for all its difficulty, was familiar. that’s okay. you’re not abandoning anything carelessly, you’re simply moving toward calmer conditions. this journey requires trust — in the person steering the boat, or in your own quiet resolve to keep paddling. you’ve got this.

## the sky

**Mercury in Scorpio** (10th house) — your mind is diving deep into what needs to be released.
**Moon in Sagittarius** (11th house) — your emotional compass points toward new horizons.
**Venus in Scorpio** (10th house) — your heart is holding onto what truly matters, even as it transforms.

## what it means together

this passage isn't just a physical shift, it’s a profound emotional and mental one, too. mercury in scorpio means you’re thinking about the core of things, stripping away the superficial to understand exactly why you’re moving on. you’re not just leaving a situation, you’re processing the *lessons* from it, deep in your bones. the moon in sagittarius, though, won't let you wallow. it pulls your gaze forward, past the wake of the boat, towards the promise of new experiences and perspectives. there’s a quiet excitement stirring, a hope for something freer, something expansive.

the bittersweet feeling of the six of swords is amplified by venus in scorpio. you’re not just letting go of old attachments, you’re transforming them. what felt like a loss might actually be a powerful redefinition of your values, a deeper understanding of what true connection means for you. this isn't about forgetting, it’s about integrating the past into a stronger future. you’re not alone on this journey, even if it feels solitary. the support is there, whether it’s from an unseen guide, a quiet friend, or the solid ground of your own inner knowing. you're heading somewhere better. trust the current.
