**Great Harvest (#14)**
*FLAME, HEAVEN, THE CLINGING, Great Harvest*

the image of great harvest is fire in heaven above — you see it, right? a brilliant flame burning high in the sky, a beacon. this isn't a hidden light, it’s not a quiet glow. it’s visible, powerful, undeniable. it demands attention. it implies a kind of dominance, a clear presence that can’t be ignored. so what does that mean for you? it means you are currently in a position of significant visibility, of clear influence. the instruction here is to use this powerful position, this undeniable light, to discern what is right and to act accordingly. you’re not just holding the light, you’re meant to guide with it.

the judgment for this hexagram tells you: *"POSSESSION IN GREAT MEASURE. Supreme success."* this isn't a subtle nudge, it’s a full-throated declaration. it’s not just about having a little more than you need, it’s about an abundance so vast it constitutes "great measure." and it’s not just success, it’s "supreme success." this isn't a time of scarcity or struggle for you, it’s a time when resources, opportunities, and perhaps even influence are flowing to you in significant quantities. this hexagram sees you as being in a powerful position, a moment where you are truly blessed with plenty. the question isn't whether you have it, it’s what you do with it.

this is a time when the universe has laid a feast at your feet — and you’re the one hosting. you have access to everything you need, and likely more than you anticipated. this calls for a particular posture: one of gratitude, yes, but more importantly, one of responsibility and stewardship. this isn’t about hoarding, it’s about managing. you're being asked to act as a steward of this abundance, to ensure it serves a greater good, or at least, that it’s handled with integrity. this hexagram isn't about the struggle to acquire, it's about the discernment required to manage what has already been acquired. it’s a moment of significant power, calling for significant wisdom.

**the changing lines**

the fourth line of great harvest says, "he makes a difference between himself and his neighbor. no blame." this isn't about superiority, it’s about clarity. with the kind of abundance and influence you’re experiencing, it’s natural, even necessary, to define your boundaries. you’re distinguishing your path, your resources, your unique position from those around you. this might mean stepping back from certain relationships or dynamics that no longer serve your expansive state, or simply acknowledging that your situation is different now. this differentiation isn't a judgment against others, it’s an act of self-definition. there’s no blame in knowing where you stand, especially when you’re standing tall.

then you arrive at the sixth line, which declares, "he is blessed by heaven. good fortune. nothing that does not further." this is the culmination, the ultimate affirmation. it’s not just a stroke of luck, it’s a deep, cosmic endorsement of your path. everything you undertake now, every action you initiate, is met with divine favor. it’s as if the universe is actively working to clear your way, to ensure your efforts bear fruit. this line isn't just about good outcomes, it’s about a profound alignment between your will and the benevolent will of heaven. your current trajectory is not only blessed, it’s unstoppable. lean into this.

## the relating hexagram

**Peace (#11) — Advance (泰)**

when the changing lines complete their movement, great harvest transforms into peace. this isn't just an end point, it’s the intended destination of all that abundance. you’re not just collecting resources, you’re moving towards a state of profound harmony and well-being. the judgment for peace is clear: *"the small departs, the great approaches. good fortune. success."* this means the petty worries, the minor irritations, the things that once felt overwhelming — they are receding. in their place, significant, beneficial, and expansive opportunities are coming into your life.

this is the resolution to the question great harvest implicitly asks: what is the purpose of all this bounty? it’s not just for having, it’s for creating a foundation of peace. the arc is clear: from possessing great measure, you move into a state of deep, underlying peace. it’s the quiet confidence that comes from knowing your resources are well-managed, your path is blessed, and your life is moving in a direction of calm prosperity. this hexagram isn't just about the absence of conflict, it’s about an active, flowing harmony where everything is exactly as it should be. this is where you're headed.

the hexagram of great harvest provides the structural layer underneath all of this, confirming and deepening what the tarot sees. you are, in fact, in a position of great measure, even if the tarot shows you struggling to fully realize or manage it. the six of swords, your quiet departure, aligns perfectly with great harvest's fourth line: "he makes a difference between himself and his neighbor." you are deliberately creating distance from the past emotional discord (ten of cups reversed) and the feeling of destitution (five of pentacles reversed). you’re defining your boundaries, choosing what to leave behind to make space for what’s truly yours.

the present moment, marked by the reversed five of pentacles, is where the hexagram truly resonates. you are *already* blessed with "possession in great measure," you are simply starting to acknowledge, accept, or access the help and resources that are right in front of you. it’s the pivot point where the "great approaches," as the relating hexagram of peace promises. the moon in virgo, with its practical, analytical eye, is helping you refine your daily habits, allowing you to see and utilize these overlooked resources. mercury in scorpio is probing deep, uncovering the hidden details of how you can best manage this emerging bounty.

the future, with the reversed emperor, is your challenge and your opportunity. with great harvest comes the profound responsibility of stewardship. the hexagram's image calls on the superior man to "curb evil and further good, and thereby obeys the benevolent will of heaven." this is the upright emperor, the wise and benevolent ruler. your struggle with the reversed emperor suggests you might resist taking on this mantle of authority, or find it difficult to establish the necessary structures for your abundance. the sun in scorpio's intense focus asks you to look deeply at where your power truly lies, and how you can wield it responsibly. the sixth line of great harvest assures you that you are "blessed by heaven," and "nothing that does not further" your path. this isn't just about good fortune, it’s a divine affirmation of your trajectory, provided you step into the role of wise management.

so, what does this mean for you today? you are in a moment of significant abundance, a great harvest is unfolding. your journey involves moving away from past emotional struggles and embracing the help that is now appearing. the crucial next step is to consciously step into the role of the benevolent ruler of your own life and resources. this isn't about being rigid, but about establishing clear, ethical structures for your newfound bounty. discern what needs to be kept, what needs to be given, and what needs to be released. the goal isn't just to possess, it's to create peace — a deep, abiding harmony that comes from responsible, divinely aligned action.
