**Beginning (#3)** 
*water, thunder, the abysmal*

The image of Beginning describes clouds and thunder — a stormy, turbulent scene. This isn't just a weather report; it's an instruction to approach the current situation with the understanding that it's complex, multifaceted, and potentially volatile. The combination of water and thunder suggests a moment of great change, where the usual flow (water) is disrupted by a powerful, energetic force (thunder). 

_"Difficulty at the beginning works supreme success, Furthering through perseverance. Nothing should be undertaken. It furthers one to appoint helpers."_ This judgment is saying that the current situation, though challenging, holds the potential for great success — but only if you persevere and approach it with the right mindset. The advice to "appoint helpers" suggests that this isn't a solo endeavor; success will come from collaboration or seeking guidance. The warning that "nothing should be undertaken" implies a need for caution and careful planning before taking any significant action.

Beginning names a time of initiation, of setting out into the unknown. It demands a posture of humility and openness, recognizing that the path ahead is not yet clear. This hexagram sees the situation as one that requires careful navigation, where the usual ways of doing things may not apply. It's a call to be adaptable, to seek out new alliances or perspectives, and to be patient, as the way forward will not be immediately apparent.

Line 2 says, _"Difficulties pile up. Horse and wagon part. He is not a robber; He wants to woo when the time comes. The maiden is chaste, She does not pledge herself. Ten years—then she pledges herself."_ This line warns of a period where challenges seem to mount, and there's a sense of disconnection or separation (the horse and wagon parting). The image of a man wanting to woo, but the maiden not pledging herself, suggests a need for patience and genuine intention. It's not a time for forcing outcomes or relationships; instead, it's about building a foundation for the long term. The ten years mentioned underscore the idea that true commitment and success come from enduring efforts, not quick fixes.

## synthesis
The single card, Four of Pentacles, set the tone for a day focused on security, stability, and perhaps a bit of isolation. The three-card spread, with the past showing a reversed Eight of Pentacles (suggesting a period of stagnation or disconnection from one's work or passions), the present as Eight of Swords (feeling trapped or limited in some way), and the future as Nine of Wands (nearing the end of a long challenge, with success in sight), paints a picture of moving from a place of disconnection and limitation towards a future of hard-won success.

The hexagram, Beginning, adds a layer of depth to this narrative, suggesting that the current challenges are not just personal or immediate but are part of a larger, structural shift. It confirms the tarot's indication of a need for patience and perseverance but adds that this is a time of initiation into something new, something that requires a humble and open approach. The hexagram complicates the tarot's story by introducing the idea that success is not just about overcoming personal challenges but about navigating the complexities of the situation with the right posture and alliances.

Practically, this means approaching the day with a mindset that is both cautious and open to new possibilities. It's about recognizing the potential for great change and success in the current difficulties, but also being aware of the need for careful planning and collaboration. The synthesis of the tarot and the I Ching suggests that the path forward, while challenging, is leading towards a place of greater stability and success, but it requires a willingness to navigate through complexity and uncertainty with patience and humility.
