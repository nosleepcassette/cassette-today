**Bringing Together (#45)**
*Bringing Together, LAKE, EARTH, THE JOYOUS, THE RECEPTIVE*

earth below, lake above. water sitting on top of the ground and pooling, which is the whole idea: this hexagram is about things finding each other and forming a body. it is the gentler sibling of hexagram 8, where water was over earth -- a river running -- but here the water has stopped and gathered, which makes the gathering more deliberate. the judgment says it plainly: *"GATHERING TOGETHER. Success. The king approaches his temple. It furthers one to see the great man. This brings success. Perseverance furthers. To bring great offerings creates good fortune. It furthers one to undertake something."*

notice what is doing the work. in hexagram 8, one strong line in the middle holds everything up. here, two strong lines -- fourth and fifth -- do the gathering. that is a structural difference and it changes the advice. this isn't one leader everyone orbits. it's two anchors, and the strength of the structure comes from how they relate. the "great man" isn't a guru to find. it's the situation or the person in your actual life who already has the standing to pull something together. see them. that's the instruction.

the image underneath is stranger and worth sitting with. over the earth, the lake: the superior man renews his weapons in order to meet the unforeseen. gathering is not the same as safety. the moment you bring a group together you also make a target of it, and the responsible move is to sharpen your tools before the thing you invited arrives -- not after. a lake is peaceful on the surface. it is also where everything drains toward.

three lines are changing here: 2, 3, and 4. lines one, five, and six hold their position, and holding still matters for a hexagram about assembly.

line two: letting oneself be drawn, good fortune and remains blameless, if one is sincere it furthers one to bring even a small offering. this is the line of the small offering, and it says something specific: don't make an arbitrary choice about how this happens. secret forces are at work, pulling together the people who belong together, and your job is to stop steering and start yielding. sincerity is the only currency. when the offering is small but sincere, it is received.

line three: gathering together amid sighs. nothing that would further, going is without blame, slight humiliation. this is the loneliest line in the hexagram. you want to join and the circle has already closed, so you are standing outside a thing that is happening without you. the answer is uncomfortable and correct: don't stand there being pure about it. go ally yourself with whoever is nearer the center and can get you in. the humiliation is real but it's small, and the alternative is a whole gathering that proceeds without you.

line four: great good fortune, no blame. and this is the line that's moving, which is the point of the whole reading. this is the person who gathers others in the name of something larger than himself -- not for advantage, out of unselfishness. and the work is crowned with success. everything becomes as it should be. the divine comment underneath is worth repeating to yourself: when the assembly is not about you, it works. when it is about you, it sags.

the stillness of line one -- sincerity that does not go all the way -- is the warning. confusion, then gathering. call out and one grasp of the hand is enough to turn the distress around, regret not, going is without blame. but the people are wavering because they're a large group influenced by the group. they need a center to gather around. line five says what happens when the center exists but isn't fully committed yet: some are not yet sincerely in the work, and sublime and enduring perseverance is needed. line six is the grief version -- lamenting and sighing, floods of tears, no blame -- where good intentions get misunderstood and the misunderstanding is what finally brings the other person to their senses.

which is where hexagram 48, Replenishing, comes in. wind below, water above, and the image of a pole and bucket drawn up from deep water. the judgment is blunt: *"THE WELL. The town may be changed, but the well cannot be changed. It neither decreases nor increases. They come and go and draw from the well. If one gets down almost to the water and the rope does not go all the way, or the jug breaks, it brings misfortune."* the well is what stays put while everything around it gets rearranged. and the last line is the warning -- almost to the water and the rope does not reach, or the jug breaks. the source is inexhaustible. getting to it badly is the failure, not the scarcity.

## the synthesis

the cards showed a blocked feeling, a sharp tongue, a genuine meeting, and a wish that may disappoint on arrival. the hexagram showed the structure underneath all of it: a lake over earth, two anchors, and a well that does not run dry.

the tarot is working at the scale of the individual -- your mood, your mouth, your capacity to actually be met by someone, your private relationship with wanting. the i ching is working at the scale of the assembly itself, and it has something specific to say about who does the gathering. line four gets great good fortune, and it gets it by not being about itself. line three is the warning about standing outside a circle that has already formed, and it tells you to stop being pure and go get in. those two lines together are the whole argument for what to do this week: stop waiting for the right conditions to assemble around you. find the anchor, bring what you have, and let the small sincere offering be enough.

this is also where the morning's page of cups reversed lands. the sensitivity without structure is why the gathering hasn't happened yet. the hexagram is not asking you to become less emotional -- the two of cups in the present says the feeling is not the problem, the reciprocity is available. it's asking you to get your own weather organized enough that you can actually be met by it. you cannot receive mutuality while you're hiding in the escapism.

and the relating hexagram tells you why this is worth doing. the well neither decreases nor increases. the town around it changes and the well does not. whatever you're assembling here is not a one-time event, it's a structure that will keep giving after the current mess of people and circumstances rearranges itself. the failure mode is the rope that doesn't quite reach, or the jug that breaks on the way up. don't reach for the thing you're not yet close enough to. don't bring the container you can't carry. go deep enough, hold it carefully, and the source does the rest.
