## the sky
**the moon in virgo** wants things accounted for — it's a time of attention to detail, and analysis. you're in a period of evaluation, where you're looking at the different parts of your life, and trying to make sense of them. 
**venus in leo** is shining a light on your relationships — it's a time of warmth, and generosity, but also a time of potential drama. you're being called to be more confident, and expressive in your relationships — to take risks, and be more open. 
**mars in taurus** is slowing down your pace — it's a time of patience, and persistence. you're being called to be more deliberate, and thoughtful in your actions — to take your time, and not rush into things.

## the thread
the three cards are connected by a thread of progress, and exploration. the **Six of Wands** sets the tone for the reading — you've achieved something, and now you're in a place of possibility. the **Seven of Cups** suggests that you've got a lot of options, and you're not sure which one to choose — it's a time of imagination, and fantasy. but the **Nine of Wands** brings you back down to earth — it's a reminder that you're not invincible, and you need to be careful. 
the transits are echoing this theme — **the moon in virgo** is calling for analysis, and attention to detail. **venus in leo** is shining a light on your relationships, and calling for confidence, and expression. **mars in taurus** is slowing down your pace, and calling for patience, and persistence. 
it's a complex reading — there are a lot of different threads, and themes. but the overall message is one of progress, and exploration. you're being called to be more discerning, and thoughtful — to take your time, and not rush into things.
