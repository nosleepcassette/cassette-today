*cycles, fate, turning point, change, opportunity*

you're standing at a point where the landscape is shifting, whether you asked for it or not. the Wheel of Fortune isn't about *your* effort, exactly—it's about the larger currents pulling things along. you thought you had a handle on things, didn't you? well, the universe has other plans, and sometimes those plans are just about turning the page. it’s not personal, it’s just how things work.

this is a reminder that everything moves in *cycles*. what's up comes down, what's down goes up. it means an opening might appear where you least expected it, or a situation you thought was stable suddenly isn't. it’s a *turning point*, a moment when the old order loses its grip and something new gets ready to spin into place. your job here isn't to stop the wheel, but to understand its rhythm. to brace for the ride.

## the sky

**Mercury in Libra** (1st house) — mercury in libra wants you to talk it out, fairly.
**Moon in Virgo** (12th house) — the moon in virgo wants things accounted for.
**Mars in Leo** (11th house) — mars in leo pushes for bold moves, a bit of a show.

## what it means together

the wheel is turning, and you're not entirely in the driver's seat for this one. that's the big picture. but the sky gives you some crucial tools for navigating the spin. with mercury in libra, your natural inclination will be to seek balance in the conversation around these changes—to make sure everyone gets a fair say, to weigh the pros and cons meticulously. don't get stuck in analysis paralysis though. the wheel isn't waiting for a perfect consensus.

your moon in virgo means your gut reaction to this upheaval might be to try and categorize it, to meticulously list every detail of what's happening, to bring order to the chaos. it’s a natural impulse to try and control the narrative, to account for every loose end. but some things simply can't be filed away neatly. sometimes the only "accounted for" is "it changed."

this is where mars in leo comes in. while you're processing and balancing, there's a drive within you to respond with courage, to take a stand. it's not about being reckless, but about acting with conviction, even if it feels like you're performing for an audience. let your actions be bold and clear, even when the outcome isn't. the wheel doesn't care for hesitation, but it respects a good, confident pivot. you can't stop the spin, but you can choose how you ride it.
