## the sky

**Saturn in Pisces square Mercury in Gemini** — your plans feel like they're hitting a wall, or the timing just isn't right.
**Moon in Pisces** — you're swimming in deeper waters, feeling out what your soul truly needs.
**Mars in Leo** — your actions are driven by a proud, creative fire, demanding recognition.

## the thread

The friction you've felt with the Wheel of Fortune reversed in your past — that sense of being stuck in a loop, of resisting an inevitable shift — it finds its echo in Saturn squaring Mercury. Your efforts to plan, to communicate, to even just *move* feel like they're dissolving into the Piscean fog, or getting tangled in contradictory messages. It's hard to get a straight answer, even from yourself, about what's next.

This is precisely why your Eight of Cups present is so potent. With the Moon in Pisces, you're not meant to be making logical leaps or planning grand strategies right now. You're meant to be feeling your way through it, allowing your intuition to guide you away from anything that feels less than whole. That soul-searching, that quiet departure, it's less about a destination and more about a deep, internal recalibration. You're shedding layers, letting go of the half-truths and partial fulfillments because your emotional core demands something real.

But the journey isn't without its pitfalls. As Mars blazes in Leo, demanding that your actions be seen and recognized, the Knight of Wands reversed warns against letting that proud fire become uncontrolled. The very passion that fuels your quest could, if unchecked, lead to rash decisions or sudden bursts of anger when things don't go exactly as you expect. You've left behind what wasn't working, and that's powerful. The thread now is about sustaining that momentum with intention, not just raw force. Move with the current of change, don't just kick against it. Find your rhythm.
