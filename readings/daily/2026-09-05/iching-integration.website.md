**Joyful (#58)**
*joyful, lake, the joyous*

the image of hexagram 58 shows two lakes, one resting on the other. think about it: deep water reflecting deep water, a boundless surface mirroring itself. this isn't a static image, you see, it's about connection, about resonance. it tells you this is a time for shared experience, for finding depth in what’s reflected between people. the instruction here is clear: *thus the superior man joins with his friends for discussion and practice.* it’s an invitation to engage, to speak your truth and hear others, to hone your understanding in good company. this isn't about isolated contemplation; it's about the joyous hum of collective intelligence.

the judgment reads: *the joyous. success. perseverance is favorable.* this isn't joy that just falls into your lap, you understand. it's a deep, abiding contentment that takes effort to maintain. it speaks to a moment where genuine, unforced delight is possible, but it requires you to show up for it, to keep at it. this isn't the fleeting high of a party, but the sustained warmth of true connection. success here isn't a goal achieved, but a state of being entered into—a state of open, reciprocal joy.

this hexagram names a season of openness and receptive pleasure. it’s a moment to find your footing in shared delight, to let the resonance of others uplift you, and to offer your own resonant energy in return. it asks you to consider where your joy truly comes from—is it internal, external, or a combination? it sees you standing at a point where the quality of your connections, the depth of your shared experience, directly impacts your overall well-being. this isn't about performing happiness; it's about cultivating a genuine, shared resonance.

the first line speaks of *contented joyousness. good fortune.* this is the simplest form of joy, the kind that just *is*. it's unburdened, unexamined, and utterly pure. when you find yourself in this space, you don't need to question it or analyze it. you simply receive it. this is a gift, a moment of effortless contentment that arrives without fanfare, and your only job is to let it wash over you. don't try to make it anything more than it is—just be in it.

the third line shifts the tone: *coming joyousness. misfortune.* this is the trap of anticipation, the danger of chasing a happiness that isn't quite here yet, or one that you're trying to force into being. it’s the shadow side of expectation, where the longing for joy, or the belief that it *should* be here, actually creates a barrier to experiencing the real thing. you might be reaching for something that feels like joy but lacks substance, or you're holding onto an idea of happiness that doesn't fit your current reality. this line is a warning: don't mistake the promise of joy for joy itself. don't let the pursuit of happiness blind you to what’s genuinely available now.

the fourth line brings a different kind of wisdom: *joyousness that is weighed is not at peace. after ridding himself of mistakes a man has joy.* this line tells you that true, lasting joy often follows a period of reckoning. it's not the fleeting kind, nor the anticipated kind. this is the joy that settles in after you've done the hard work of clearing the decks, of acknowledging where you've been off course, and making adjustments. it’s a quiet, deep satisfaction that comes from integrity, from aligning yourself with what’s right. it’s a peace that precedes the joy—a necessary foundation built on self-awareness and corrective action. you can't force this joy; you earn it by letting go of what's no longer serving you.

## relating hexagram 48: Replenishing (The Well)

this is where the path leads, you see. hexagram 58, Joyful, with its changing lines, culminates in hexagram 48, Replenishing, also known as The Well (井). the well is a constant, a source. *the well. the town may be changed, but the well cannot be changed. it neither decreases nor increases. they come and go and draw from the well.* where hexagram 58 is about the active experience of shared joy and connection, hexagram 48 is about the deep, unchanging source that sustains it all. it's the foundation beneath the lakes, the water from which everything draws nourishment. your journey from active, resonant joy (58) moves towards finding and protecting that fundamental, internal source (48). after the shifts and the releases, you arrive at the essential, unchanging core. what are you drawing from? what truly replenishes you, independent of external circumstances? this is your landing point, your sustained source.

## synthesis

the single card, the wheel of fortune, sets the tone for your day: things are in motion. there’s a turn happening, a shift in the currents of your life. this isn't something you control, but something you navigate. the hexagram, 58, Joyful, then asks: how do you find your footing, your resonant place, amidst this change? it’s not about resisting the wheel, but finding the shared delight that grounds you as it turns.

your three-card spread offers a personal narrative within this larger movement. in the past, the wheel of fortune reversed shows a resistance to change, a feeling of being stuck, perhaps even fighting against the inevitable turning of that very wheel. this echoes line 3 of hexagram 58: "coming joyousness. misfortune." that resistance to the natural flow, that yearning for a joy that isn't quite landing because you're out of sync with the shifting reality. you were perhaps trying to force the wheel to stop, or spin your way.

now, in the present, the eight of cups arrives. this is the quiet walk away. it’s the conscious decision to leave behind what no longer serves, even if it means stepping into uncertainty. this card beautifully mirrors line 4 of hexagram 58: "joyousness that is weighed is not at peace. after ridding himself of mistakes a man has joy." the eight of cups is that "ridding himself of mistakes," that necessary release of the old, the worn, the joyless. it's the difficult but essential work that precedes true, earned contentment. you are making space for what’s real.
