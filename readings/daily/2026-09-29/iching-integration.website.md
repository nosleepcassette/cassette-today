**Growing Upward (#46)**
*Growing Upward, EARTH, WIND, THE RECEPTIVE*

within the earth, wood grows — that’s the image for pushing upward. think about it: a seed, deep in the dark, finding a sliver of moisture, a pocket of air, and then just *pushing*. no fanfare, no grand announcement, just a persistent, quiet, upward striving. it’s about the roots, the unseen work, the slow, undeniable force of life making its way to the light. this isn't a sudden leap or a dramatic conquest; it’s an organic, internal expansion, a dedicated commitment to growth from the ground up. you are in a season of building something substantial, from the inside out.

the judgment promises, "pushing upward has supreme success. one must see the great man. fear not. departure toward the south brings good fortune." this isn’t a tentative wish; it's a declaration. you’re on the right path, heading for real success. "seeing the great man" means connecting with a higher vision, a guiding principle, or perhaps someone who embodies the wisdom you need right now. it's about aligning your personal push with a larger, more noble purpose. and that "fear not" is a direct message to you — the natural anxieties that come with any ambitious, long-term project are acknowledged, then dismissed. move into the light, move into action, and trust the process.

this hexagram names a time of steady, incremental ascent. it’s not about shortcuts, but about the accumulated power of consistent effort. you're being asked to cultivate patience, to understand that true growth takes time, like a tree adding rings year after year. your ambition right now is a quiet hum, a deep-seated drive that doesn't need external validation to keep going. it sees the value in every small step, knowing that each one contributes to something high and great. your task is to keep tending to those small things, to believe in the upward current even when you can’t see the crown of the tree yet.

line 2 tells you, "if one is sincere, it furthers it to bring even a small offering." this is about the integrity of your effort, not its size. you don't need to make a grand gesture or present a fully finished masterpiece. what matters is the truth in your dedication, the genuine heart you bring to the task, however minor it seems. a small offering, given sincerely, carries more weight than a large, hollow one. it's a reminder that your quiet, consistent work, fueled by a true belief in what you're doing, is powerful. don't underestimate the impact of your honest commitment, even in its humblest forms.

then line 3 arrives: "one pushes upward into an empty city." this line hits a little differently, doesn't it? you're making progress, you’re ascending, you're doing the work — but maybe the applause isn't there. perhaps the people you expected to be waiting aren't. it’s a solitary phase, a moment where your efforts might not be immediately recognized or celebrated by the outside world. it’s not a sign of failure; it’s just a particular experience of growth. you’re building, you’re moving, but the echoes of your footsteps are the only sound for a while. this is a test of your internal resolve, a call to find your motivation from within, independent of external validation. the city might be empty now, but your upward push is still valid, still necessary.

the single card, the eight of pentacles reversed, sets a peculiar tone for your morning. it speaks to a potential disengagement from the very meticulous, steady work that hexagram 46 champions. you might be feeling burnt out, questioning the value of all this effort, or perhaps just plain frustrated with the slow pace. there's a sense that your craft, your dedication, isn't paying off in the way you hoped, or you’re simply not finding joy in the grind anymore. it's a moment of asking: *is this worth it?*

when hexagram 46 completes its movement, it becomes the receptive (#2) 坤. the arc of your day, then, is from an active "pushing upward" to a state of profound, receptive perseverance. the judgment for the receptive speaks of "sublime success, furthering through the perseverance of a mare." this is where your growth is leading you: to a place where the greatest strength is in following, in enduring, in being responsive rather than forcefully leading. after the pain and the pushing, you find connection, partnership, and emotional reciprocity. this isn't about *forcing* a relationship or a desired outcome, but about being open to it once you've done the internal work. it's the fruit of the "perseverance of a mare" – a harmonious union that arises when you've cultivated your inner self and allowed things to unfold naturally.

so, what does this mean for you today? don't give up on the quiet work. the eight of pentacles reversed is a warning against abandoning the very process that will lead you to sublime success. your dedication, even when it feels unrecognized or painful, is the root system for something great. trust the slow, upward push. be sincere in your small offerings, those quiet acts of commitment that you might not even notice adding up. acknowledge the current heartache, that "empty city" feeling, but know that it’s a phase, not your destination. the ultimate success isn't about leading by force, but about a deep, receptive connection that will arrive when you've allowed yourself to grow. keep tending your roots. the connection is coming.
