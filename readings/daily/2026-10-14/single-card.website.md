*impulsiveness, recklessness, anger, inconsistency, burnout, frustration*

The Knight of Wands is fire in motion -- charisma and urgency and that specific kind of forward momentum that doesn't ask if the timing's right, it just goes. Upright, he's compelling. He's the call to adventure that actually works. But reversed, the same charge that makes him magnetic becomes what burns him out. Passion outrunning judgment. Motion without direction. The horse is spooked and the knight is just holding on.

What this looks like in practice: you have the desire, the spark, the thing you want to do or say or become -- but it's moving faster than you can track, or it keeps spiking and crashing, or it's expressing itself as irritation because the energy has nowhere clean to land. That's not failure. That's fire looking for a container.

This is a card about not wasting the heat. The Knight reversed doesn't mean the fire goes out -- it means it needs a direction before it burns through its own fuel. The urgency you're feeling is real. The need to aim it first is also real. Those two things aren't in conflict.

## what it means together

There are days where the sky and the cards are telling the same story from different angles. And then there are days like today -- one card, no transits standing out, and that's enough. Sometimes the reading is clean and singular. Sometimes a single reversed knight is the whole message.

The Knight of Wands reversed is asking you to slow down just enough to notice where you're actually pointing. Not because the energy is wrong -- it isn't -- but because recklessness and courage aren't the same thing. The frustration you might be sitting with today, whatever form it's taking, is data. It's showing you what you care about. It's locating the heat.

The work isn't suppression. It's redirection. Find the thing worth burning for, then burn for it on purpose.
