*burden, responsibility, effort, pressure, obligation*

You are carrying more than other people can see. The Ten of Wands is the long final stretch of a road: ten sticks, one figure, still walking. It doesn't ask whether the work matters. It asks whether you're carrying it alone.

The load is real. Money, duty, promises you made before you knew what they'd cost. Each addition was reasonable when it happened. Now they're all on your back at once, and you're still moving, which is either admirable or a very specific kind of collapse. Probably both.

Scorpio is sitting on your second house right now, twice over — sun and Mercury — which means the weight is attached to what you own, what you're worth, and how you talk about both. You don't want a light load. You want a load with a reason attached. That's a different kind of exhaustion. It keeps you up. It makes you check the numbers again.

So here's the first thought: this is too much. Here's the real thought: you already know which piece of it you could set down. You've known for a while.

## the sky

**Sun in Scorpio** (2nd house) — the sun in your second house burns off the version of you that other people approved of, and wants your worth, your money, and your self-image stripped down to something you actually recognize.

**Moon in Aries** (7th house) — the moon in your seventh house wants your relationships to be direct and alive, and gets short-tempered with anything that asks you to tiptoe.

**Mercury in Scorpio** (2nd house) — mercury in your second house thinks in locked rooms, researches what it doesn't want to admit it already knows, and says the second thing instead of the first.

## what it means together

The card and the sky are talking about the same thing from different floors. Ten of Wands says: too much, carried alone. Scorpio in your second house says: and you're not sure the value you're carrying is even yours. Someone else's standard. A version of security that looks fine from the outside and feels like a locked door from the inside. When your sun and Mercury are both digging at your resources and your sense of worth, the bundle gets heavier, not lighter, because now you're carrying the question of whether you deserve to set it down.

The moon in aries in your seventh house is the relief valve. Not a rescue. Aries doesn't rescue. It charges. It wants you to say the thing directly — to a partner, a friend, a client, the mirror — and it wants that conversation to happen now, before the pressure hardens into resentment. Direct doesn't have to mean cruel. It can mean: I am carrying this, and I am done carrying it alone.

So the practical read: name one obligation out loud, to another human being, and see what falls off. Then check whether what's left is yours. If it is, keep it. It probably is. The Ten of Wands isn't a warning against ambition. It's a warning against pretending the weight is free.
