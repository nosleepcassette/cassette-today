*jealousy, volatility, insecurity, selfishness, domination*

you know that feeling when you're standing in front of a fire you lit yourself, and instead of warming your hands you're just watching it burn everything you built? that's this card. the queen of wands upright is a force of nature — she knows what she wants, she goes after it, she fills a room without trying. reversed, that same fire turns inward. the confidence curdles into control. the passion becomes jealousy. you're still magnetic, but now you're using that pull to keep people close so they can't leave first.

this isn't about being bad. it's about being scared. the queen reversed is what happens when someone who's used to leading suddenly feels the ground shift under them. you lash out. you hold tighter. you mistake attention for affection, and you start measuring your worth by how many people are looking at you. the irony is that the more you demand the spotlight, the more you push people out of its glow. you end up alone in the center of a very hot, very empty room.

## the sky

no transits to speak of today. the sky is quiet — nothing major pulling the strings, no planets throwing elbows. just you and this card. which means the heat you're feeling? it's yours. all yours.

## what it means together

so there's no cosmic excuse. no mercury retrograde to blame, no mars square to point at. the queen of wands reversed is showing you a mirror, and the mirror is asking: *what are you so afraid of losing?* because that's what the volatility is about — not anger, not dominance, but the terror of being forgotten. of being average. of the fire going out and nobody noticing.

the fix isn't to put the fire out. it's to stop using it as a weapon. charisma without generosity is just a hostage situation. you don't need to be smaller — you need to be warmer. let people come to the flame because they want to, not because you're blocking the exit. that's the work of this card: trusting that your heat will attract what it needs without you having to scorch everything else first.
