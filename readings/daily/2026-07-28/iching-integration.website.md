**Already Fulfilled (#63)** — water over fire, the abysmal over the clinging. the image is literal: water sits on fire, the cooking is done. but that's not a resting state — water will eventually put the fire out, or the fire will boil the water dry. completion is a temporary equilibrium, not a permanent condition. the judgment says it straight: *after completion. success in small matters. perseverance furthers. at the beginning good fortune. at the end disorder.* the whole thing is a warning dressed as a blessing. you got what you wanted. now what.

the first line breaks your wheels before you even get moving. “he breaks his wheels. he gets his tail in the water.” you're trying to roll forward, but something jams the axle. you're already in over your head — tail wet, stuck. this isn't a failure, it's a check. the hexagram says don't rush the next move. the thing you just finished? it's still settling. pushing harder now just gets you wet.

the third line is a longer arc. “the illustrious ancestor disciplines the devil's country. after three years he conquers it. inferior people must not be employed.” that's a campaign, not a skirmish. something you've been fighting — an external obstacle, maybe an internal one you've been calling external — takes time. three years is symbolic, but the point is patience and selectivity. you can't bring everyone along. some hands don't belong on the tools. this line is asking who you're letting into your process. if they're not aligned, they'll drag the whole thing down.

together, lines one and three say: don't force the start, and don't trust the wrong people. the day is already full of small completions — the danger is treating them as permissions to accelerate or delegate carelessly.

the relating hexagram is **8: Union (Holding Together)** — the movement from already fulfilled to union is not a step back, it's a consolidation. after the water settles over the fire, what remains is the need to gather. the judgment for union says *holding together brings good fortune. inquire of the oracle once again whether you possess sublimity, constancy, and perseverance; then there is no blame.* the arc: you completed something. now you have to hold the people who helped you get there — or find the ones who belong in the next phase. the primary hexagram's discipline (line three) clears the field. the relating hexagram says okay, now who's left? hold them.

## the sky

**Moon in Scorpio (8th house)** — emotional intensity around what's been buried, what's owed, what's shared. not a light transit. the moon here wants to go deep, but the hexagram says you're already in the deep end. don't swim harder — tread.

**Sun in Leo (10th house)** — the completion is visible. people see it. the sun in the house of career, status, public life says the thing you finished has an audience. but the queen of wands reversed says you don't feel like performing. that's fine. you don't have to.

## synthesis

the queen of wands reversed is the morning's tone: you're not feeling the fire. the queen upright is radiant, confident, leading from the gut. reversed, she's depleted, second-guessing, maybe a little resentful that the thing you worked for doesn't feel like a victory. the three-card arc backs this up: past three of cups — you had your people, you celebrated, you were in the flow. present five of wands — now you're in a scrum. competing voices, conflicting priorities, maybe just the friction of completion itself. future two of pentacles — you'll be juggling. adaptability required. no rest.

the hexagram sees the same structure but from underneath. already fulfilled is the condition of the day, but the tarot says you're not experiencing it as fulfillment. the queen reversed is the disorder at the end that the judgment warned about. the five of wands is the wheels breaking on line one — you try to move forward and the axle snaps. the two of pentacles is the three-year campaign of line three: you'll be balancing competing demands for a while. but the relating hexagram — union — is the landing. you don't get through this alone. the three of cups in the past wasn't just a nice memory; it was a template. the present conflict (five of wands) and the future juggling (two of pentacles) both get easier when you stop treating them as solo problems.

practical: don't push. your wheels are already broken — trying to roll anyway will just grind the axle. instead, look at who's around you. the illustrious ancestor didn't conquer the devil's country alone; the line says *inferior people must not be employed*, which is a roundabout way of saying: employ the right ones. the union hexagram says hold together. call someone. let the five of wands be a conversation, not a war. the queen of wands reversed isn't a sign you've lost your fire — it's a sign you've been running on fumes. the hexagram says the thing is done. you can stop now.
